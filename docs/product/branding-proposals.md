# Branding Proposals — App Name and Mascot

**Status:** Proposal, owner decision needed · **Owner:** pm · **Date:** 2026-11-06

> **Decision (2026-11-07):** Adopted **Polyglow** with mascot **Lumi**, palette midnight
> indigo + amber/gold. See **PD-001** in `decisions.md`. The trademark, domain and handle
> check is still waiting on the owner (non-blocking). The proposals below are kept as the
> original record.

## Ground rules (non-negotiable)

- Nothing derived from Duolingo or any other language app: not the name, the owl or any
  bird mascot that looks like it, green-as-brand colour, illustration style, fonts,
  sounds or content.
- **No owls and no green-led palette.** Green may be used only as a functional "correct
  answer" colour inside the lesson UI, never as the brand colour.
- All names below are **working proposals only**. **Trademark, domain and app-store name
  availability has not been checked.** The owner must run a trademark search (USPTO,
  EUIPO, WIPO Global Brand Database), check domains and social handles, and get legal
  advice before adopting any name.

## Name options

### 1. Polyglow  (RECOMMENDED)
- **Rationale:** "poly" (many, as in polyglot) plus "glow" (the warm feeling of progress,
  and a streak that "keeps glowing"). Short, easy to say in English and Spanish, and it
  ties directly to the recommended mascot and the streak mechanic. Works for future
  languages because it isn't Spanish-specific.
- **Caveat:** "Poly-" names are common. There may be existing brands in cosmetics,
  lighting or software using the word. **Trademark and domain availability must be
  checked by the owner.**

### 2. Wordsprout
- **Rationale:** language as something you grow a little every day. Fits the habit loop,
  where each lesson is a bit of growth. Descriptive and friendly.
- **Caveat:** a sprout or plant theme pulls the visuals toward green, which we must not
  use as the brand colour. It would need a non-green palette (for example terracotta and
  cream) to stay clearly distinct. Longer name, and the compound may already be in use
  by education products. **Trademark and domain availability must be checked by the
  owner.**

### 3. Tandu
- **Rationale:** a short, invented, brandable word that suggests "tandem" (learning
  side by side) and is easy to pronounce in many languages. Invented words are usually
  easier to protect as trademarks.
- **Caveat:** says nothing about language learning, so it needs more marketing to
  explain. Could mean something in a language we haven't checked. Needs a
  linguistic/cultural check as well as a **trademark and domain check by the owner.**

## Mascot concepts

### A. Lumi, the paper lantern  (RECOMMENDED)
- **Concept:** a small, round paper lantern with a soft face drawn on the paper and two
  stubby paper-fold arms. A warm light inside shows Lumi's mood and the learner's
  progress.
- **Ties to mechanics:**
  - **Streak:** Lumi's glow gets brighter and warmer as the streak grows. A broken streak
    dims Lumi to a gentle ember that relights after the next lesson, which keeps the
    feedback kind rather than guilt-driven.
  - **Daily goal:** the progress ring fills like light spreading around the lantern.
  - **Results screen:** Lumi floats up a little and gives off sparks.
- **Visual direction:** deep indigo or midnight-blue background with warm amber, apricot
  and soft gold light. Flat shapes with a subtle paper texture and rounded geometric
  forms. Not an animal and not a bird, so it can't be confused with existing
  language-app mascots.
- **Why it fits Polyglow:** the name and mascot tell one story: learning makes you glow.
- **Cultural note:** paper lanterns appear in many cultures. Keep the design generic
  (simple rounded shape, no specific festival or religious motifs) and review it with a
  diverse group before finalising.

### B. Mo, the hermit crab
- **Concept:** a cheerful, curious hermit crab who wears a new shell for every unit (a
  café cup for the food lesson, a tiny suitcase for travel). Curious, a bit clumsy, and
  never scolds.
- **Ties to mechanics:** collecting shells could become an achievement system in
  Phase 2. Mo pokes out of the shell for correct answers and hides briefly for incorrect
  ones.
- **Visual direction:** coral and sand tones with ocean-teal accents (teal, not green).
- **Trade-off:** a fun, extensible theme, but less tied to the name options, and an
  animal mascot is a more crowded space.

## Recommendation

**Name: Polyglow · Mascot: Lumi, the paper lantern.**
**Brand palette direction:** midnight indigo + amber/gold, with a warm off-white
background. Functional lesson colours (correct/incorrect) chosen separately for WCAG AA
contrast.

Next steps for the owner:
1. Run trademark, domain and handle checks on "Polyglow" (and the backups).
2. Confirm or pick an alternative, then replace `[APP_NAME]` and `[MASCOT]` in
   `CLAUDE.md`.
3. Commission or design original mascot artwork (MVP needs only static poses: idle,
   happy, dim/ember, celebrating).
