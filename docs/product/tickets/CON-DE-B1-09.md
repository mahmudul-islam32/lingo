## User story
As a learner, I want to understand how things are done in my town and talk about what I
do for the environment, so that I can follow notices about waste and building work and
join local activities.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** energy and saving, sorting waste, building work, traffic and transport, a
  neighbourhood clean-up
- **Life in DACH:** sorting glass, paper and organic waste, returning bottles for the
  deposit, a new cycle path, getting a bike repaired, a Saturday clean-up in the park
- **Can-do:** I can say what is done and by whom, talk about what I do for the environment,
  understand simple notices about building work, and say what I have done by someone else.
- **Grammar:** **passive present** (`wird / werden` + participle) with **`von` + dative**
  for the agent; `man` as the active alternative; **`lassen` + infinitive** (`Ich lasse mein
  Rad reparieren.`).

## Lesson outline
1. `de-b1-u09-l1-environment` **"The Environment"**: `Wir sparen Energie.`, `Ich schalte den
   Computer aus.`, `Der Kühlschrank verbraucht viel Strom.`, `Die Natur ist hier schön.`,
   `Die Umwelt ist uns wichtig.`, `Das ist umweltfreundlich.` `das Licht` is **not** allowed
   (not taught).
2. `de-b1-u09-l2-is-sorted` **"The Rubbish Is Sorted"**: `Das Glas wird gesammelt.`, `Das
   Papier wird getrennt.`, `Der Biomüll wird am Montag abgeholt.`, `Für die Flaschen bekommt
   man Pfand.`, `Das Plastik wird in die gelbe Tonne geworfen` is **not** allowed (`die
   Tonne` alone not taught); use `in die Mülltonne`.
3. `de-b1-u09-l3-built-by` **"Built by the City"**: `Die Brücke wird von der Stadt gebaut.`,
   `Ein neuer Radweg wird gebaut.`, `Die Straße ist gesperrt.`, `Die Bibliothek wird um neun
   geöffnet.`, `Wann wird die Straße wieder geöffnet?`, `Die Baustelle ist laut.`
4. `de-b1-u09-l4-by-bike` **"By Bike or by Bus?"**: `Ich fahre mit dem Rad zur Arbeit.`, `Es
   gibt viel Verkehr.`, `Wir stehen im Stau.`, `Die Luft ist schlecht.`, `Der Lärm stört
   mich.`, `Ich nehme die öffentlichen Verkehrsmittel.`, `Ich lasse mein Rad reparieren.`,
   `Lässt du dein Auto waschen?`
5. `de-b1-u09-l5-clean-up` **"Scenario: The Neighbourhood Clean-Up"**: a notice and a chat
   between neighbours (du): `Am Samstag gibt es eine Aktion im Park.`, `Machst du mit?`, `Die
   Aktion ist freiwillig.`, `Die Handschuhe werden von der Stadt gebracht.`, `Der Müll wird
   getrennt.`; a short report in Präteritum afterwards (`Viele Leute kamen.`).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-b1-u09`, title "Environment and the City", description "Talk about the
   environment and your town, say what is done with the passive, and what you have done with
   lassen.", in level B1 after `de-b1-u08`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-b1-u09-l1-environment` "The Environment"
   2. `de-b1-u09-l2-is-sorted` "The Rubbish Is Sorted"
   3. `de-b1-u09-l3-built-by` "Built by the City"
   4. `de-b1-u09-l4-by-bike` "By Bike or by Bus?"
   5. `de-b1-u09-l5-clean-up` "Scenario: The Neighbourhood Clean-Up"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-28** pass.
3. **Passive present** appears in at least 10 exercises across L2–L5 with at least 6
   different participles and both `wird` and `werden`. At least 1 MC offers `Das Glas wird
   gesammelt.` with the distractors `Das Glas wird sammeln.` and `Das Glas werden gesammelt.`
4. **`von` + dative** for the agent appears in at least 4 exercises across L3–L5. At least 1
   MC has the wrong-case distractor `von die Stadt`.
5. The active `man` version appears in at least 2 exercises. Passive drills leave `man` out
   of the tiles (CDS-23.3).
6. **`lassen` + infinitive** appears in at least 5 exercises across L4–L5. At least 1 MC has
   the distractor `Ich lasse mein Rad zu reparieren.` `lassen` appears only in the present
   (CDS-24.4).
7. **Recycling (CDS-25):** U4 verbs with prepositions or `da-` compounds in at least 2
   exercises; U5 `um … zu` in at least 2 (`um Energie zu sparen`); U8 `damit` in at least 2;
   U2 Präteritum in at least 2 (L5 report).
8. The environment is about personal everyday actions: no statistics, no climate or
   political claims, no statement of waste or deposit rules or amounts (CDS-26).

| # | Lesson | New items (de → en) | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Environment | die Umwelt (the environment), die Natur (nature), die Energie (energy), sparen (to save), der Strom (electricity), verbrauchen (to use / consume), umweltfreundlich (environmentally friendly) | 7 |
| 2 | The Rubbish Is Sorted | *pattern:* passive present (wird / werden + participle); das Glas (glass), das Papier (paper), das Plastik (plastic), der Biomüll (organic waste), das Pfand (the deposit, on bottles), sammeln (to collect), werfen: du wirfst / geworfen (to throw) | 7 |
| 3 | Built by the City | *pattern:* passive + von + dative (by …); bauen (to build), die Brücke (the bridge), der Radweg (the cycle path), öffnen (to open), schließen / geschlossen (to close), die Baustelle (the building site / roadworks), gesperrt (closed / blocked off) | 7 |
| 4 | By Bike or by Bus? | *pattern:* lassen + infinitive (to have something done); lassen: du lässt (to have something done / to let), das Rad (the bike, short for Fahrrad), der Verkehr (the traffic), der Stau (the traffic jam), die Luft (the air), der Lärm (the noise), die öffentlichen Verkehrsmittel (public transport) | 7 |
| 5 | The Neighbourhood Clean-Up | die Aktion (the campaign / event), mitmachen (to join in / take part), freiwillig (voluntary), die Handschuhe (the gloves) | 4 |

Unit total: 32 items. `der Müll`, `die Mülltonne`, `trennen`, `stören` (A2 U9),
`ausschalten` (A2 U13), `die Flasche`, `das Fahrrad`, `das Auto` (A1), `geöffnet /
geschlossen` (A2 U12, now also as passive participles) are taught. `der Kühlschrank` is A1
U10.

**Not yet taught (don't use in this unit):** `wurde` and the passive Präteritum (U12), the
passive with modals (`muss repariert werden`, B2), `das Licht`, `die Tonne`, `der Container`,
`das Klima`, Konjunktiv II beyond the U1 chunks (U10), genitive (U11).

## Out of scope
- Passive Präteritum (U12), passive with modal verbs and state passive (B2)
- Climate policy, statistics, political debate (CDS-26)
