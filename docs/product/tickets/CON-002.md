# CON-002: Multiple-choice prompts in English, options in Spanish (Unit 1 fix)
**Owner:** pm · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** CON-001, LES-004 · **Milestone:** v0.2.0 · **Branch:** `fix/con-002-mc-prompt-direction`

Found in the cto review of LES-004: the multiple-choice component marks the prompt with
`lang={knownLanguage}` and every option with `lang={learningLanguage}` (the contract has
no per-option language). Seven Unit 1 exercises asked the reverse ("Which one means
'ocho'?" with English options), so screen readers read English options with a Spanish
voice and the Spanish word with an English voice. Fixed in content only, no contract
change (PD-019).

_Note: the roadmap's earlier v1.0.0 line for CON-002 (QA pass, speak exercises, final
audio references) moves to a new content ticket, CON-004, planned with v1.0.0._

## User story
As a learner using a screen reader, I want every multiple-choice question read in
English and every answer option read in Spanish so that I hear each word pronounced in
its own language.

## Acceptance criteria
1. In all 5 Unit 1 lesson files, every `multiple_choice` prompt is English, in the form
   "Which one means '<English word or phrase>'?".
2. Every `multiple_choice` option text in Unit 1 is Spanish (Unit 1 vocabulary from the
   CON-001 appendix).
3. Each changed exercise keeps its `id`, has 3–4 options with unique ids and texts, and
   its `correctOptionId` points to the Spanish translation of the prompt word.
4. Every word whose Spanish → English recognition was tested only by a reversed
   multiple choice is still tested in that direction by a `word_bank_translate`
   `to_known`, `match_pairs` or `listen` exercise in the same lesson.
5. Each lesson still has 10–12 exercises and the CON-001 criterion 3 mix (at least 3
   multiple choice, at least 3 word bank with both directions, exactly 1 match pairs, at
   least 2 listen).
6. Numbers stay split as in PD-013 (1–3 in "At the Café", 4–10 and "¿cuántos?" in
   "Count It"); no lesson uses a word from a later lesson.
7. `course.json` `version` is bumped (1 → 2) so cached lessons refresh.
8. `npx vitest run src/contracts/__tests__/content.test.ts` and the Lesson 1 integration
   test (`tests/integration/lesson-flow.test.tsx`) pass.

## Out of scope
- A per-exercise or per-option language field in the contract (raise a contract request
  only if a later unit needs English options; PD-019)
- Speak exercises, final audio references, full content QA pass (CON-004, v1.0.0)
- `word_bank_translate` prompts: `to_known` prompts are Spanish by design and the
  component uses `direction` to set the language

## UX notes
- No new screens or states. Multiple-choice screen: prompt (English) on top, 3–4 Spanish
  option cards; loading, error and feedback states are unchanged from LES-004.
- Prompt wording stays "Which one means '<word>'?" (CON-001 UX notes); prompts at most
  80 characters.
