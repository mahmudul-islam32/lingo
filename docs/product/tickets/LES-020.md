# LES-020: Earn hearts on correct answers, free practice, out-of-hearts copy
**Owner:** dev-lessons · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** CTO-015 (contract, ADR-013),
GAM-011 (`gainHearts`, `useGainHearts`), LES-009 · **Milestone:** v0.5.2 · **Decisions:**
PD-012, PD-018, PD-065

**ID note:** LES-016 is reserved for prompt audio (PD-055) and LES-017 … LES-019 for the B1
components (CTO-010), so this ticket takes the next free number, LES-020.

## User story
As a learner, I want each correct answer to give me back a heart and finished lessons to be
free to replay, so that practising keeps me going instead of waiting for a refill (US-HE-01,
US-HE-02, US-HE-03).

## Acceptance criteria
Prop and function names follow ADR-013 / CTO-015; the behaviour is what is tested.

### Earning hearts in a lesson
1. Each "Check" with a correct result raises the header hearts by 1 at once (up to 5) and
   calls `useGainHearts` exactly once. At 5 hearts nothing changes and the hook is not
   called.
2. A re-queued exercise answered correctly later earns 1 heart like any correct Check.
3. A match-pairs exercise that completes with no mismatch earns 1 heart; with any mismatch it
   costs 1 heart (LES-009 criterion 5) and earns none.
4. Speak exercises and skipped exercises never change hearts.
5. A gain is announced in a polite live region: "You earned a heart. N hearts." Nothing is
   announced at 5.
6. A gain plays a short animation on the meter (at most 400 ms); under
   `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the number changes with no animation.
7. A heart from the timed refill during a lesson is still not added (LES-009 criterion 14).
8. If saving a gain fails, the lesson goes on with the in-memory count and the failure is
   logged (as LES-009 criterion 13).

### Practice (completed lesson)
9. `HeartsLessonPlayer` accepts a practice flag (set by the route, PLT-025). In practice,
   wrong Checks and match-pair mismatches cost **no** hearts and `useLoseHearts` is never
   called; wrong exercises are still re-queued and counted as mistakes (PD-012).
10. In practice, correct Checks still earn hearts (criteria 1–6).
11. A practice lesson starts at 0 hearts (the LES-009 start gate does not apply) and never
    ends with the out-of-hearts screen.
12. In practice the header shows a "Practice" badge next to the hearts meter, with the
    accessible text "Practice: wrong answers cost no hearts".
13. A practice lesson saves its result and earns XP as any replay (PD-008).
14. Without the practice flag, losing hearts and the start gate work exactly as in LES-009
    (its tests pass unchanged).

### Out-of-hearts screen
15. The out-of-hearts screen (LES-009 criterion 8, both the start gate and the mid-lesson
    end) shows the line "Practise a finished lesson to earn hearts." under the refill lines
    when the learner has at least one completed lesson; without one the line is absent. The
    primary button stays "Back to Learn".

### Quality
16. RTL tests cover: 3 hearts → correct → 4 and one gain call; 5 → correct → 5, no call;
    re-queued correct; match pairs with and without mismatch; speak and skip; practice
    at 0 hearts with a wrong answer (no loss, re-queued) then a correct answer (1 heart);
    practice result saved; the badge text; live-region texts; reduced motion; the
    out-of-hearts line with and without completed lessons.
17. No serious or critical axe violations in the player header with the practice badge and
    on the out-of-hearts screen.
18. At 375 × 667 the header (quit button, progress bar, practice badge, hearts meter) fits on
    one row without truncating the meter, and the out-of-hearts screen with the new line fits
    without scrolling with the button pinned (at least 48 px).
19. e2e (Playwright, 375 px, keyboard only): with 0 hearts and a completed lesson, open it,
    answer one exercise correctly with Tab and Enter, and see the header at 1 heart; then
    "Back to Learn" shows 1 heart in the top bar.

## Out of scope
- Mounting the practice flag in the lesson route (PLT-025)
- The path dialog copy and the path's 0-hearts gate (GAM-011)
- Sound on gaining a heart (LES-012, v1.0.0)
- Rewards beyond hearts for practice (Phase 2)

## UX notes
- **Heart gained:** the meter's heart briefly grows and the number rises; no confetti.
- **Practice:** small "Practice" badge (outlined, token colours) left of the meter; no other
  change to the lesson flow.
- **Out of hearts:** unchanged layout; the new line sits under the two time lines, before
  the reassurance line.
- **Loading / error:** unchanged from LES-009 (hearts read error → full hearts).
- **Empty:** not applicable.
