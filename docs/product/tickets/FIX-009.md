# FIX-009: Path auto-scroll keeps level and unit headers clear of the top bar
**Owner:** dev-gamification · **Priority:** P1 (blocked the v0.5.3 merge) · **Depends on:**
GAM-012, FIX-002, FIX-006 · **Milestone:** v0.5.3 Redesign · **Found in:** review of the
GAM-012 screenshots

## User story
As a learner opening Learn, I want the next lesson centred in the space I can see, with no
level or unit header half hidden behind the top bar, so that the path reads cleanly.

## Problem
The redesign added a sticky top bar (course chip, streak, XP, hearts). The load-time
auto-scroll centred the next node in the whole viewport, so a level header card could end
up half under the top bar (`redesign-v2.png`: "A1 · Beginner" cut off).

## What was fixed
- `scroll-to-center.ts`: `stickyInsets` measures the sticky and fixed bars;
  `centredScrollTop` centres in the visible area and adjusts so no header card
  (`[data-path-header]`) is left partly under the top bar.
- `SkillPathView.tsx` passes the header cards to `scrollToCenter`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. After load, the next node is centred in the visible area between the top bar and the
   bottom nav.
2. No level or unit header card is partly covered by the top bar after the auto-scroll.
3. Tab order still starts at the top of the page (FIX-002), and the `settled` signal still
   fires (FIX-006).
4. Unit tests for `stickyInsets`, `centredScrollTop` and the header guard; e2e in
   `gamification-loop.spec.ts` and `start-level.spec.ts`.

## Review
**PM (PM-ACC4, 2026-11-20): accepted.** 1-4 PASS. Screenshots `final-A1.png` and
`final-A2.png` show the header card fully below the top bar. Tests are in
`scroll-to-center.test.ts` and `SkillPathLevels.test.tsx`, and e2e is 142/142.
