## User story
As a learner, I want to tell and understand short stories about the past in written
German, so that I can read a biography or a letter and tell people about my own life.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** childhood, family history, moving, how a couple met
- **Life in DACH:** a grandmother's story about the village she grew up in, a short
  biography in a neighbourhood newsletter, a couple telling how they met
- **Can-do:** I can tell a short story in the past in written style, understand a short
  biography, and say when something happened once (`als`) or every time (`wenn`).
- **Grammar:** **Präteritum** of regular verbs (`-te / -ete`, all persons); 12 irregular and
  mixed forms as appendix items; Perfekt in speech vs Präteritum in writing (CDS-23.1);
  **`als`** (one time in the past) vs **`wenn`** (every time).

## Lesson outline
1. `de-b1-u02-l1-back-then` **"Back Then"**: regular Präteritum: `Früher lebte meine
   Großmutter in einem Dorf.`, `Damals arbeitete mein Großvater in einer Bäckerei.`, `Sie
   wohnte zwanzig Jahre in Österreich.`, `Ihre Kindheit war schön.`, `Er lernte Englisch.`
   `jeden Tag` is **not** allowed (`jeder` is U3); use `oft`.
2. `de-b1-u02-l2-went-came-saw` **"Went, Came, Saw"**: `Sie kam neunzehnhundertsiebzig nach
   Deutschland.`, `Es gab kein Wasser im Haus.`, `Er fand eine Stelle in Hamburg.`, `Sie nahm
   den Zug nach Wien.`, `Wir blieben drei Wochen.`, `Er sah das Meer.`, `Sie fuhr mit dem Bus
   zur Arbeit` is **not** allowed (`die Arbeit` is U4); use `zum Büro`.
3. `de-b1-u02-l3-first-then` **"First, Then, Finally"**: story order: `Zuerst wohnte sie bei
   ihrer Oma.`, `Danach begann sie eine Ausbildung.`, `Plötzlich kam ein Brief.`, `Endlich
   hatte sie eine Wohnung.`, `Am Anfang war alles neu.`, `Am Ende war sie glücklich.`, `Sie
   schrieb viele Briefe.` No `Am Ende des …` (genitive is U11).
4. `de-b1-u02-l4-when-i-was-a-child` **"When I Was a Child"**: `Als ich ein Kind war, wohnten
   wir in einem Dorf.`, `Wenn wir Ferien hatten, fuhren wir zu meiner Oma.`, `Jedes Mal, wenn
   es schneite, spielten wir draußen.`, `Der Unterricht begann um acht.`, `Ich war erst
   zehn.`, `Das war wirklich schön.` Real city names outside DACH are **not** allowed (CDS-3).
5. `de-b1-u02-l5-how-we-met` **"Scenario: How We Met"**: a couple (invented names) tells a
   friend how they met in a course: narration in Präteritum (`Wir lernten uns im Kurs
   kennen.`, `Ich wusste sofort: …`, `Ich dachte, er ist Lehrer.`, `Zwei Jahre später
   heirateten wir.`) and the friend's questions in Perfekt (`Wo habt ihr euch kennengelernt?`).
   `der Deutschkurs` only in a listen or MC option (CDS-22.9).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-b1-u02`, title "Life Stories", description "Tell and understand stories about
   the past with the Präteritum, and say when things happened with als and wenn.", in level
   B1 after `de-b1-u01`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-b1-u02-l1-back-then` "Back Then"
   2. `de-b1-u02-l2-went-came-saw` "Went, Came, Saw"
   3. `de-b1-u02-l3-first-then` "First, Then, Finally"
   4. `de-b1-u02-l4-when-i-was-a-child` "When I Was a Child"
   5. `de-b1-u02-l5-how-we-met` "Scenario: How We Met"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-28** pass.
3. **Regular Präteritum** appears in at least 10 exercises with at least 6 different verbs
   and the `ich`, `er / sie`, `wir` and `sie` (they) forms, including `-ete` (`arbeitete`). At
   least 1 MC has the wrong-form distractors `wohnete` and `arbeitte`.
4. **Each of the 12 irregular forms** in the appendix appears in at least 2 exercises. At
   least 1 MC offers `ging` with the distractors `gehte` and `gang`; at least 1 offers `kam`
   with `kommte`.
5. **Register (CDS-23.1):** L1–L4 are narration (Präteritum canonical). L5 has at least 2
   dialogue exercises in the Perfekt. Every `to_learning` tile set builds only one past
   tense, or accepts both. No MC marks a correct Perfekt as the error.
6. **`als` vs `wenn`** appear in at least 6 exercises across L4–L5. At least 1 MC contrasts
   `Als ich ein Kind war, …` with `Wenn ich ein Kind war, …` for a one-time situation, and 1
   uses `jedes Mal, wenn …`. Clause-first and main-clause-first orders are both accepted
   (CDS-24.2).
7. Every gap word of this unit (`früher`, `damals`, `leben`, `zuerst`, `danach`,
   `plötzlich`, `endlich`, `beginnen`, `der Anfang`, `das Ende`, `wirklich`, `erst`,
   `kennenlernen`, `heiraten`, `wusste`, `dachte`) appears in at least 2 exercises.
8. **Recycling (CDS-25):** U1 polite chunks in at least 2 exercises (L5 dialogue); A2 years
   in words in at least 2; A2 `weil` in at least 2.
9. Stories are warm, invented and everyday: moving for work or family, school, a first
   flat. No war, flight, hardship history, residence status or real events (CDS-26).

## Vocabulary appendix
| # | Lesson | New items (de → en) | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Back Then | *pattern:* regular Präteritum (-te / -ete: lebte, wohnte, arbeitete); früher (in the past / used to), damals (back then / at that time), leben / lebte (to live, be alive; live somewhere), das Dorf (the village), die Kindheit (childhood), die Großmutter (the grandmother), der Großvater (the grandfather) | 7 |
| 2 | Went, Came, Saw | ging (went, Präteritum of gehen), kam (came), sah (saw), fuhr (went / drove / travelled), es gab (there was / there were), fand (found; also thought, opinion), nahm (took), blieb (stayed) | 8 |
| 3 | First, Then, Finally | zuerst (first / at first), danach (after that / afterwards), plötzlich (suddenly), endlich (finally / at last), beginnen / begann / begonnen (to begin / began / begun), schrieb (wrote), der Anfang / am Anfang (the beginning / at the beginning), das Ende / am Ende (the end / in the end) | 8 |
| 4 | When I Was a Child | *pattern:* als (one time) vs wenn (every time) in the past; als (when, one time in the past; new meaning), wenn (whenever / every time, in the past; new meaning), jedes Mal (every time, fixed chunk), der Schüler / die Schülerin (the pupil / student at school), die Ferien (the school holidays), der Unterricht (lessons / class), wirklich (really), erst (only / not until: Ich war erst zehn.) | 8 |
| 5 | How We Met | kennenlernen / lernten … kennen / kennengelernt (to meet / get to know), sich verlieben / verliebt (to fall in love), heiraten / geheiratet (to marry), die Geschichte (the story), wusste (knew, Präteritum of wissen), dachte (thought, Präteritum of denken) | 6 |

Unit total: 37 items. Separable and prefixed verbs built on a listed form are allowed
(`kam … an`, `fuhr … ab`, `nahm … mit`; CDS-22.1). `war`, `hatte` and the modal Präteritum
are A2. `alles` is from A1 `das ist alles`; `viele` from A2 `viele Grüße`; `sofort` from A1
`ab sofort`.

**Not yet taught (don't use in this unit):** other irregular Präteritum forms (`sprach`,
`las`, `aß`, `traf`, `rief`, `lief`, `half`, `trug`, `wurde`), `jeder / jeden` except in `jedes
Mal`, `die Arbeit` (U4), relative clauses (U3), Plusquamperfekt and `nachdem` (U12),
genitive (U11), `die Tante`, `der Onkel`, `zum ersten Mal`, `ans Meer`.

## Out of scope
- Plusquamperfekt, `nachdem`, `bevor` (U12); passive Präteritum (U12)
- Präteritum of every irregular verb: only the appendix forms
- Family history involving migration law, war or hardship (CDS-26)

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "Stories and letters use the Präteritum: Sie lebte in einem Dorf. Regular
  verbs add -te: wohnte, lernte; -ete after t or d: arbeitete."; "als = one time in the past:
  Als ich zehn war, … wenn = every time: Wenn es schneite, …"
- The L5 story is told by two voices in the audio script notes (one voice per listen
  exercise; two voices are Phase 2, PD-034).
