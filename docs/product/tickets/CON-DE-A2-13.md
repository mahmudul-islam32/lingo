## User story
As a learner, I want to compare things, say what I like best and talk about the media I use,
so that I can share opinions in everyday conversations.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** phones, apps, the internet, TV, news, radio and newspapers; comparing and
  preferring; screen time
- **Life in DACH:** the evening news, a family discussion about screen time
- **Can-do:** I can compare two things, say what I like best, say which media I use and how
  often, and give a short opinion.
- **Grammar:** **comparative** `-er … als` (umlaut: `älter`, `größer`, `wärmer`), `so … wie`;
  **superlative** `am …-sten` (predicative only); irregular `gut – besser – am besten`,
  `gern – lieber – am liebsten`, `viel – mehr – am meisten`; `als` vs `wie`.

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u13-l1-phone-and-internet` **"Phone and Internet"**: `Ich chatte abends mit meiner
   Schwester.`, `Die App ist neu.`, `Mein Laptop ist kaputt.`, `Bist du online?`, `Ich lese
   die Nachrichten auf dem Handy` is **not** allowed (`die Nachrichten` = news is L3); use
   `Ich schreibe eine Nachricht.` (U8 `die Nachricht`).
2. `de-a2-u13-l2-faster-and-cheaper` **"Faster and Cheaper"**: `Der Laptop ist schneller als
   der Computer.`, `Das Handy ist billiger.`, `Mein Bruder ist älter als ich.`, `Im Sommer ist
   es wärmer.`, `Die App ist so gut wie die Zeitung.`, `leicht`, `schwer`, `praktisch`.
3. `de-a2-u13-l3-best-of-all` **"Best of All"**: `Ich sehe am liebsten Krimis.`, `Diese
   Sendung ist am besten.`, `Wer liest am meisten?`, `Im Winter ist es am kältesten.`, `die
   Nachrichten`.
4. `de-a2-u13-l4-news-and-opinions` **"News and Opinions"**: `Ich lese die Zeitung online.`,
   `Im Radio kommen um acht die Nachrichten.`, `Meiner Meinung nach ist die Werbung zu laut.`,
   `Ich finde, dass der Artikel interessant ist.` (U11), `Das stimmt.`
5. `de-a2-u13-l5-screen-time` **"Scenario: Screen Time"**: a family talks about how long the
   children use their phones and agrees on a rule: `die Bildschirmzeit`, `weniger`, `zu
   viel`, `ausschalten`, `einschalten`; imperatives (U7) and `wenn` (U12) recycled.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u13`, title "Media and Comparisons", description "Talk about phones, news and
   TV, compare things and say what you like best.", in level A2 after `de-a2-u12`, with these
   lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u13-l1-phone-and-internet` "Phone and Internet"
   2. `de-a2-u13-l2-faster-and-cheaper` "Faster and Cheaper"
   3. `de-a2-u13-l3-best-of-all` "Best of All"
   4. `de-a2-u13-l4-news-and-opinions` "News and Opinions"
   5. `de-a2-u13-l5-screen-time` "Scenario: Screen Time"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. Comparatives appear in at least 6 exercises, including at least 2 with umlaut and at least
   1 with `so … wie`.
4. Superlatives `am …-sten` appear in at least 4 exercises, including `am besten` and `am
   liebsten`.
5. At least 2 multiple choices contrast `als` and `wie` (`größer als` vs `so groß wie`), and
   no answer accepts the colloquial `größer wie`.
6. No attributive comparatives or superlatives (`der schnellere Zug`, `das beste Handy`): B1.
7. No brand names of devices, apps, platforms, channels or newspapers (CDS-8).
8. Opinions recycle `dass` (U11) in at least 2 exercises and `meiner Meinung nach` with the
   verb straight after it in at least 1.

Unit total: 31 items. `besser` (U7), `lieber` and `mehr` (A1) are now part of the system.
`die Handynummer` (A1) shows `das Handy`; `die Nachricht` (U8, message) vs `die Nachrichten`
(news) is pointed out in the L3 tip.

## Out of scope
- Attributive comparatives and superlatives (B1)
- `je … desto` (B2)
- Media literacy, politics, news content about real events (B1+)

  groß wie."
