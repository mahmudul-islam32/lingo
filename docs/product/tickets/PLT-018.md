# PLT-018: Onboarding "I already have an account" path
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** PLT-010, PLT-013, PLT-014, PLT-017 (log-out flow, criterion 6 only), PLT-004 · **Milestone:** v0.4.0

## User story
As a returning learner on a new device, I want to log in from the first screen so that I
continue with my streak, XP and daily goal instead of starting over (US-PLT-06,
US-PLT-05; ends PD-011).

## Context
A brand-new device is a guest **without settings**. Logging in therefore needs no
migration (PLT-013 criterion 2): the device adopts the account and pulls its data. What
this ticket adds is the entry point and the routing that follows: skip onboarding if the
account was already onboarded, finish it otherwise.

## Acceptance criteria
1. When accounts are enabled (PLT-010 criterion 2), the onboarding welcome step shows a
   secondary "I already have an account" button under "Get started". In `off` mode it
   isn't rendered (the PD-011 behaviour stays).
2. The button opens `/login?from=onboarding`. The log-in screen's "Continue as guest"
   link then goes back to the onboarding welcome step instead of `/`.
3. After a successful log-in (email, Google or password reset) from this path, the
   handover (PLT-013) adopts the account, and a full-screen "Loading your progress…"
   (Lumi, `role="status"`) shows until the first pull has finished.
4. If the pulled account has a profile with `onboardedAt` **and** settings, the learner
   lands on Learn and sees the account's goal, total XP, streak and path. Onboarding
   doesn't show again on this device.
5. If the account has no settings yet (created but never onboarded anywhere), the
   learner continues onboarding at the course step. The chosen course and goal become
   the account's settings and are synced (one `settings` sync record, then accepted by
   the demo backend).
6. If the first pull fails, the screen shows "We couldn't load your progress." with "Try
   again" (primary) and "Log out and continue as a guest" (secondary: PLT-017's log-out
   flow, then the welcome step). It never drops the learner into onboarding with blank
   settings, because their fresh `updatedAt` would win over the account's real settings under
   last-write-wins (PD-024).
7. The log-in screen's offline note (PLT-010 criterion 15) applies, and the welcome
   button stays visible offline.
8. Keyboard: the new button comes after "Get started" in tab order. The loading and
   error screens announce their state. There are no serious or critical axe violations on
   the welcome step (with the new button) and on the loading and error screens. The
   welcome step still fits 375 × 667 without scrolling, with both buttons visible.
9. Playwright (demo mode, mobile): an account is created and onboarded with goal 30 and
   Lesson 1 completed in one browser context. In a fresh context: "I already have an
   account" → log in → "Loading your progress…" → Learn with goal "15 / 30 XP", total XP
   15 and Lesson 1 completed. A second test logs in with a never-onboarded account and
   lands on the course step.

## Out of scope
- Signing up from the welcome step (guests sign up after their first lesson, PLT-013,
  per G1's "first lesson within 2 minutes")
- Choosing between this device's guest data and the account's (a fresh device has none;
  otherwise PD-024 merges)

## UX notes
- **Welcome step:** "Get started" (primary, unchanged) and under it "I already have an
  account" (secondary or ghost button, full width).
- **Loading:** full screen, Lumi idle, "Loading your progress…".
- **Error:** Lumi dim, message, two stacked buttons.
- **Success:** Learn (criterion 4) or the course step (criterion 5). No toast: a returning
  learner expects to see their progress.
