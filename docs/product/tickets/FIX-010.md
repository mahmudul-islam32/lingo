# FIX-010: Results streak line once, gold daily-goal bar, audio load timeout
**Owner:** dev-lessons · **Priority:** P1 (blocked the v0.5.3 merge) · **Depends on:**
LES-021, LES-010, LES-014 · **Milestone:** v0.5.3 Redesign · **Found in:** review of the
LES-021 screenshots and audio testing

## User story
As a learner finishing a lesson, I want each result line shown once and the daily-goal bar
in the XP colour, and I want "Play audio" to fall back to speech quickly on a slow network,
so that the results screen is clear and audio never seems stuck.

## Problem
1. "Streak started!" appeared twice: in the gold pill under the heading and in the coral
   streak banner (`redesign-v2.png`, `les-sheet.png`).
2. The daily-goal bar used a dark olive fill instead of the design's XP gold.
3. On a slow or stalled network, "Play audio" stayed busy for several seconds before the
   speech fallback.

## What was fixed
- `celebrationPillText`: the gold pill shows only "Perfect lesson! +N bonus XP". There is no
  pill when the lesson isn't perfect. Streak and goal events stay in their own rows. The
  polite live region is emptied after it has been announced, so browse mode reads each line
  once.
- Goal bar fill `xp-gold` (#F5B400) with an `xp` (#8A6200) edge, which keeps 3:1 against the
  track and the card (WCAG 1.4.11).
- `AUDIO_LOAD_TIMEOUT_MS = 3000`: a clip that hasn't started within 3 s counts as `failed`,
  and the existing speech fallback runs.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Each of "Streak started!" / "Streak extended!" / "Daily goal reached!" is shown once on
   the results screen, in its row. The gold pill shows only the perfect-lesson line.
2. The daily-goal bar fill is `xp-gold` with an edge of at least 3:1 against the track and
   the white card (unit test).
3. A clip that hasn't started within 3 s resolves as `failed` and falls back to speech (unit
   test with a fake timer).
4. `lessons-results.spec.ts` and `gamification-loop.spec.ts` are updated and pass.
