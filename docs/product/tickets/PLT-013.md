# PLT-013: "Save your progress" prompt and guest → account handover
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** PLT-010, PLT-014, PLT-009, PLT-016 (profile card slot) · **Milestone:** v0.4.0

## User story
As a guest, I want to be asked to save my progress after my first lesson, and I want my
XP, streak, completed lessons and daily goal moved to my account when I sign up or log
in, so that I don't lose anything (US-PLT-04, US-PLT-05; PRD metric "guest → account
conversion ≥ 25 %").

## Context
`completeSignIn` (PLT-010) is the one step every sign-in path runs: email sign-up and
log-in, Google (PLT-011) and password reset (PLT-012). This ticket fills it in with the
**account handover**. The handover uses the `migrate_guest_to_account` RPC (ADR-003 §5)
through the backend gateway, then switches the device's identity to the account, then
pulls with the sync engine (PLT-014). The demo backend implements the same merge rules
as the RPC. Product rules: PD-024 (merge) and PD-025 (prompt).

## Acceptance criteria
**Handover decision**
1. If the device identity is a **guest with saved settings** (onboarding done), the
   handover builds a `GuestMigrationPayload` (schema-valid) from Dexie with the guest
   id, profile, settings, stored hearts and **every** lesson result. It sends the
   payload once through the gateway's migrate call.
2. If the device identity is a **guest without settings** (onboarding not done, so no
   results are possible), no migration call is made. The identity switches to the account
   and the pull runs (PLT-018 relies on this).
3. If the device identity already is this account (`userId` matches), nothing is
   migrated and the learner goes to Learn.
4. Auth screens are only reachable while signed out (PLT-010 criterion 16), so a
   different account is never already on the device. If it happens anyway (e.g. stale
   tabs), the handover stops with "You're already logged in to another account on this
   device. Log out first." plus a "Back to Learn" button. Nothing is migrated or deleted.

**Success**
5. After the migrate call succeeds, in this order: the local identity becomes
   `{ kind: "account", userId }`, the guest's pending sync records are removed (their
   content is already on the server), then `syncNow()` runs and its pull merges remote
   facts (PLT-014). The progress, settings, profile and hearts queries are invalidated.
6. Guest data is never deleted before the migrate call has succeeded (tested by making
   the fake fail at each step).
7. **New account:** after the handover, Learn shows exactly the guest's previous totals:
   same total XP, streak, daily goal progress and completed lessons. Profile shows the
   guest's join date (`joinedAt` kept).
8. **Existing account with its own progress (other device):** after the handover, the
   lesson results are the union (no duplicates, no double XP: total XP = sum over
   distinct result ids). Settings, profile and hearts follow "newer `updatedAt` wins".
   The join date is the earlier of the two. Tested with the demo backend's merge.
9. Replaying the same payload, for example after a lost response, changes nothing on the
   server (idempotent; demo backend tested like ADR-003 §5).
10. A toast "Your progress is saved to your account." shows once on Learn after a
    handover that migrated data. A sign-in with nothing to migrate (criterion 2 or 3)
    shows no toast.

**Failure**
11. While the handover runs, a full-screen state shows Lumi and "Saving your progress…"
    (`role="status"`), with no other actions.
12. If the migrate call fails (network, 5xx or rejected payload), the screen shows "We
    couldn't save your progress yet." with "Try again" (primary) and "Continue for now"
    (secondary, → Learn). The device stays a guest with all its data. The learner stays
    signed in.
13. A signed-in session on a guest device (a failed or unfinished handover) retries the
    handover automatically on the next app start and on the next `online` event, until it
    succeeds. Lessons finished in between are included in the retry payload, because it
    is rebuilt from Dexie each time.
14. If the pull after a successful migration fails, the handover still counts as done
    (identity = account). The sync engine retries the pull on its normal schedule, and the
    learner goes to Learn with the toast.

**"Save your progress" prompt (PD-025)**
15. When accounts are enabled (PLT-010 criterion 2), the device is a guest, it is online,
    and the guest has at least 1 lesson result, Learn shows a dialog the first time the
    learner returns to Learn after completing a lesson: Lumi (happy pose), the heading
    "Save your progress", the text "Create a free account to keep your streak and XP safe
    on any device.", and the buttons "Create account" (primary, → `/signup`), "Log in"
    (→ `/login`) and "Not now".
16. The prompt shows at most once per local day and at most 3 times in total. "Not now",
    Escape and the close button all count as a dismissal. It never shows in `off` mode,
    while offline, during a lesson, or to a signed-in learner. The counters are device UI
    state and survive a reload. Rules unit-tested with injected dates.
17. Profile (PLT-016) shows a guest-only card "Save your progress" with the same text
    and the "Create account" and "Log in" buttons, whenever accounts are enabled. It
    ignores the prompt counters, and is hidden for accounts and in `off` mode.
18. After sign-up or log-in started from the prompt or the card, the handover runs and the
    learner ends on Learn (not on the auth screen).

**Quality**
19. The prompt is a modal dialog with a focus trap, a labelled heading, Escape to close,
    and focus returned to Learn's main heading on close. The handover screens announce
    their status. There are no serious or critical axe violations (prompt, saving, error).
    No horizontal scrolling at 375 × 667, and the dialog buttons fit without scrolling at
    375 × 667.
20. Playwright (demo mode, mobile):
    - **New account:** a guest completes Lesson 1 (15 XP, streak 1), returns to Learn,
      sees the prompt, presses "Create account", signs up, sees "Saving your progress…",
      then Learn with the toast, total XP 15, streak 1 and Lesson 1 completed. A reload
      keeps the same values.
    - **Existing account:** a demo account seeded with 1 result for Lesson 1 on "another
      device". A guest on this device completes Lesson 1 and logs in. Learn shows both
      results' XP (sum of 2 distinct results) and Lesson 1 completed once on the path.
    - **Failure:** with the demo migrate call set to fail once, the error screen shows.
      "Try again" then succeeds.

## Out of scope
- Asking the learner which progress to keep (always merge, PD-024)
- Moving data from an account back to a guest
- A sign-up event for analytics (PLT-019, v1.0.0)
- Name or avatar changes during sign-up (PD-026)

## UX notes
- **Prompt (dialog):** bottom sheet on mobile, centred dialog from 640 px. Lumi happy pose
  80 px, heading, one sentence, stacked full-width buttons ("Create account", "Log in"),
  then a text button "Not now".
- **Loading (handover):** full screen, Lumi idle, "Saving your progress…", no spinner
  overlay on top of Learn.
- **Error:** same screen, Lumi dim pose, the message and two buttons. No technical error
  text.
- **Success:** Learn with the toast. The path, top bar and goal ring show the merged
  values.
- **Profile card:** a card at the top of Profile, under the header, same copy as the
  prompt. It has no "Not now" (it's not an interruption).
