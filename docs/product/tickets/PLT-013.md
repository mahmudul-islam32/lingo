# PLT-013: "Save your progress" prompt and guest → account migration
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** PLT-010, CTO-025 (incl. its
criterion 2 on the merge rule) · **Milestone:** v0.6.0 Accounts and sync, part A (local
Supabase) · **Decisions:** PD-085, PD-087

## User story
As a guest, I want to be asked to save my progress after my first lesson, and to keep my XP,
streak, completed lessons, goal date and review history when I create an account or log in, so
that I never lose what I've done (US-PLT-04, US-PLT-05).

## Acceptance criteria
### Prompt
1. With accounts enabled and online, a guest who finishes their **1st** lesson, and again their
   **5th**, sees a "Save your progress" dialog after the results screen's "Continue": "Create an
   account to keep your XP, streak and goal date on every device." with "Create account"
   (primary, to `/sign-up`) and "Not now". After those two times it never opens by itself
   (Profile keeps the entry, PLT-016).
2. The dialog never opens during a lesson, offline, for an account, or with accounts disabled.
   "Not now", Esc and the close button dismiss it and return focus; dismissal is stored on the
   device.

### Migration (`finishSignIn()`)
3. After a successful sign-up or log-in (email or Google) on a device whose identity is a
   **guest with a settings record**, the app builds a `GuestMigrationPayload` from Dexie
   (profile, settings incl. `startLevel` and `goalPlans`, hearts, **every** lesson result incl.
   `kind` and `exerciseOutcomes`), validates it with the schema and calls
   `migrate_guest_to_account`.
4. **Nothing to merge (PD-087):** on **log-in**, a guest with no lesson results is not migrated;
   its local data is replaced by the account's (pull, PLT-015). A guest without a settings record
   (not onboarded) is never migrated; the learner continues as the account.
5. While it runs: "Saving your progress…" (`role="status"`) with Lumi; the learner can't start a
   lesson meanwhile.
6. **Success:** the identity becomes `account` (`userId` = the auth user), the guest's queued
   sync records are removed (they were in the payload), a pull runs (once PLT-015 is merged) and
   "Your progress is saved." is shown with "Continue" to `/`.
7. **Nothing is lost:** an e2e compares before and after migration on the same device: total XP,
   streak, lessons done, path frontier, hearts, daily goal, start level, the goal plan of each
   course and the Review card count are equal; the account has one `lesson_results` row per
   local result.
8. **Failure** (network, 5xx, timeout): nothing changes locally, the identity stays guest and
   "We couldn't save your progress yet. It's safe on this device." is shown with "Try again".
   The app retries by itself on the next start or `online` event while the session exists.
9. **Idempotent:** a retry after a lost response inserts no duplicate result (RPC dedupe, test
   with the same payload twice).
10. A device whose identity is already an account never migrates again; logging in to another
    account requires log-out first (PLT-017).
11. Into an **existing** account with its own progress, results are unioned and every total is
    recomputed from the union; settings follow ADR-020 (CTO-025 criterion 2). e2e: account with
    lessons A, B; guest with lessons B', C; after log-in Learn shows A, B and C done and total XP
    = sum of all four results. Until PLT-015 is merged this is checked in the database (the
    account holds the union); the screen check is part of PLT-015's e2e.

### Quality
12. Dialog: `role="dialog"`, labelled by its heading, focus trapped, 48 px buttons, fits at 375 ×
    667 without scrolling. No serious or critical axe violations in the dialog, saving, success
    and failure states. Unit tests for the payload builder and the criterion 4 rules;
    `npm run typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, `build` pass.

## Out of scope
- Pull and second-device merge (PLT-015); the sync engine (PLT-014)
- Choosing which device's settings win in a UI (ADR-020 decides the rule)

## UX notes
- **Loading:** criterion 5. **Error:** criterion 8. **Success:** criterion 6, a calm moment with
  Lumi, no confetti. **Empty:** n/a.
