## User story
As a learner living in a German-speaking country, I want to understand the main point of an
official letter and what I'm asked to do, so that I can reply, bring the right documents
and pay on time without asking someone to translate.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** official letters, applications and documents, reasons and delays, a payment
  reminder, phoning the office
- **Life in DACH:** an invented letter from the citizens' office about a new ID card, a
  reference number and enclosures, a payment reminder from a sports club, a call to the
  clerk in charge
- **Can-do:** I can understand the main point of a short official letter and what I am asked
  to do, say whose documents are missing, give reasons with `wegen`, and ask about a letter
  on the phone.
- **Grammar:** **genitive basics**: `des / eines / meines` + `-(e)s`, `der / einer / meiner`,
  plural `der`; `wessen?`; names with `-s` (`Annas Pass`); **`wegen`, `trotz`, `während` +
  genitive**; `von` + dative accepted as the spoken alternative (CDS-23.7).

## Lesson outline
1. `de-b1-u11-l1-official-letters` **"Official Letters"**: `Ich habe ein Schreiben vom Amt
   bekommen.`, `Der Bescheid ist da.`, `Wie ist das Aktenzeichen?`, `Die Anlage fehlt.`, `Wer
   ist der Absender?`, `Die Behörde antwortet schriftlich.` `oben` is **not** allowed (not
   taught).
2. `de-b1-u11-l2-whose` **"Whose Form Is It?"**: `Das ist der Pass meines Mannes.`, `Wessen
   Antrag ist das?`, `Das ist Annas Antrag.`, `Ich beantrage einen neuen Personalausweis.`,
   `Die Geburtsurkunde meiner Tochter fehlt.`, `Der Pass ist nicht mehr gültig.`
3. `de-b1-u11-l3-because-of` **"Because of the Delay"**: `Wegen der Verspätung komme ich
   später.`, `Trotz des Regens gehen wir zu Fuß.`, `Während des Kurses ist das Handy ausgeschaltet.`,
   `Wegen einer Krankheit konnte ich nicht kommen.`, `Die Bearbeitung dauert zwei Wochen.`,
   `Ihr Antrag wird bearbeitet.`
4. `de-b1-u11-l4-reminder` **"A Payment Reminder"**: an invented reminder from a sports
   club: `Das ist eine Mahnung.`, `Die Rechnung ist noch offen.`, `Bitte überweisen Sie den
   Betrag bis spätestens Freitag.`, `Ich habe das Geld schon überwiesen.`, `Die Zahlung ist
   angekommen.`
5. `de-b1-u11-l5-calling-the-office` **"Scenario: Calling About a Letter"**: a formal call:
   `Ich möchte wegen des Schreibens nachfragen.`, `Wer ist mein Sachbearbeiter?`, `Können Sie
   mir sagen, welche Unterlagen fehlen?`, `Ich reiche die Kopie nach.`, `Muss ich den Antrag
   persönlich einreichen?`, `Ich lasse eine Kopie machen.`

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-b1-u11`, title "Letters from the Authorities", description "Understand official
   letters, say whose documents they are with the genitive, and give reasons with wegen.",
   in level B1 after `de-b1-u10`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-b1-u11-l1-official-letters` "Official Letters"
   2. `de-b1-u11-l2-whose` "Whose Form Is It?"
   3. `de-b1-u11-l3-because-of` "Because of the Delay"
   4. `de-b1-u11-l4-reminder` "A Payment Reminder"
   5. `de-b1-u11-l5-calling-the-office` "Scenario: Calling About a Letter"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-28** pass.
3. **Genitive articles and endings** appear in at least 8 exercises across L2–L5:
   masculine and neuter with `-(e)s`, feminine, and plural. Names with `-s` appear in at
   least 2. At least 1 MC offers `der Pass meines Mannes` with the distractors `der Pass
   meinem Mann` and `der Pass meines Mann`.
4. **`wegen`, `trotz`, `während`** appear in at least 6 exercises across L3–L5, each at least
   twice. At least 1 MC has the dative distractor `wegen dem Regen`, which is never accepted
   (CDS-23.7; on the native-review list).
5. `wessen?` appears in at least 2 exercises. Genitive drills leave `von` out of the tiles;
   where `von` + dative is buildable, it is accepted (CDS-23.7).
6. At least 4 `to_known` word banks across L1, L4 and L5 translate one line of an invented
   letter (sentence-level reading until B1-T3 exists).
7. **Recycling (CDS-25):** U1 polite chunks and indirect questions in at least 2 exercises
   each (L5); U9 passive present in at least 2; U9 `lassen` in at least 2; U6 dative or
   prepositional relative clauses in at least 2 (`die Behörde, bei der …`).
8. **Safety (CDS-26, PD-035):** letters are invented and come from generic offices (`das
   Amt`, `die Behörde`, `das Bürgeramt`) or a club; no residence, visa, citizenship, benefit
   or tax content; no deadlines, fees, fines or consequences stated as rules; the reminder
   names no late fees.

| 2 | Whose Form Is It? | *pattern:* genitive: des / eines / meines + -(e)s; der / einer / meiner; names + -s; wessen? (whose?), der Antrag (the application), beantragen (to apply for), die Geburtsurkunde (the birth certificate), der Personalausweis (the identity card), gültig (valid) | 6 |
| 3 | Because of the Delay | *pattern:* wegen / trotz / während + genitive; wegen (because of), trotz (despite / in spite of), während (during, preposition), die Krankheit (the illness), bearbeiten (to process / deal with), die Bearbeitung (the processing) | 6 |
| 4 | A Payment Reminder | die Mahnung (the payment reminder), überweisen / überwiesen (to transfer, money), die Überweisung (the bank transfer; new meaning, "referral" is U7), der Betrag (the amount), spätestens (at the latest), die Zahlung (the payment), offen (open / outstanding) | 7 |
| 5 | Calling About a Letter | der Sachbearbeiter / die Sachbearbeiterin (the clerk / case worker), nachfragen (to ask / enquire), einreichen (to submit / hand in), nachreichen (to hand in later) | 4 |

Unit total: 31 items. `das Bürgeramt`, `das Formular`, `ausfüllen`, `die Unterschrift` (A1
U12), `die Unterlagen`, `der Pass`, `die Kopie`, `unterschreiben`, `die Bescheinigung`,
`fehlen`, `schriftlich`, `persönlich` (A2 U12), `die Rechnung` (B1 U8) are taught.

**Not yet taught (don't use in this unit):** `wurde` / passive Präteritum (U12), genitive
after adjectives (`des neuen Antrags`, U13), `oben`, `unten`, `die Frist`, `die Gebühr`,
`das Finanzamt`, `die Ausländerbehörde` and any residence or benefit vocabulary (CDS-26).

## Out of scope
- Residence, visa, citizenship, benefits and taxes (PD-035, CDS-26)
- Reading whole letters: reading passage type B1-T3 and CON-DE-B1-RDG later
- Genitive with adjective endings (U13)

## UX notes

  genitive: wegen der Verspätung, trotz des Regens."
- Letters in audio are read in a neutral voice; no official logos or real addresses.
