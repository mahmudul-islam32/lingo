# GAM-001: Lumi mascot component (4 static poses)
**Owner:** dev-gamification · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** CTO-004 · **Milestone:** v0.1.0

## User story
As a learner, I want a friendly guide character on key screens so that the app feels warm
and encouraging (supports US-GAM-13; brand per PD-001).

## Acceptance criteria
1. A shared component `<Lumi />` in `/src/components/ui/` accepts `pose`
   (`"idle" | "happy" | "ember" | "celebrating"`) and `size` (`"sm" | "md" | "lg"` =
   48 / 96 / 160 px).
2. Lumi is drawn as original inline SVG: a small round paper lantern with a face and a warm
   amber/gold glow, indigo outlines. It has no animal, bird or owl features, and uses no
   third-party, stock or traced artwork.
3. The four poses differ visibly: **idle** (calm face, normal glow), **happy** (smile,
   brighter glow), **ember** (sleepy face, dim glow, for missed streak / offline / out of
   hearts), **celebrating** (big smile, strongest glow, small sparkle shapes).
4. By default Lumi is decorative (`aria-hidden="true"`). When a `label` prop is passed, it
   renders with `role="img"` and that `aria-label`.
5. Poses are static in MVP (PD-001). No animation, so nothing changes under
   `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. The outline has at least 3:1 contrast against the warm off-white background token.
7. Colours come from design tokens, not literals inside the component logic.
8. Each pose's SVG markup is 5 KB or less.
9. Unit tests render every pose at every size, check the default `aria-hidden`, and check
   the `role="img"` + `aria-label` when `label` is set.
10. The pm checks originality and brand fit before acceptance (Review section).

## Out of scope
- Animations and reactions (Phase 2)
- Mapping streak state to a pose (GAM-009, v0.3.0)
- Avatar presets for profiles (PLT-016)

## UX notes
- Used on: onboarding welcome (happy), offline fallback (ember), results screen
  (celebrating), empty states (idle).
- Must read clearly at 48 px (top bar / cards) and at 160 px (hero).
- **Loading/empty/error/success:** not applicable to the component itself; it is the
  illustration used inside those states elsewhere.
