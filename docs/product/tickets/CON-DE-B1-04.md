## User story
As a learner with a job in a German-speaking team, I want to talk about my tasks, what I'm
waiting for and what I'm looking forward to, so that I can follow meetings and handle
everyday work talk.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** tasks, meetings, shifts, problems with office equipment, end-of-day small talk
- **Life in DACH:** a team meeting, a shift plan, a printer that won't print, a chat at the
  coffee machine before `Feierabend`
- **Can-do:** I can talk about my tasks, say what I'm waiting for, thinking about or looking
  forward to, ask what someone is talking about, and report a problem at work.
- **Grammar:** **verbs with fixed prepositions** and their case; **`wo-` compounds** in
  questions about things and **`da-` compounds** in answers; preposition + pronoun for
  people (`Auf wen wartest du? – Auf sie.`).

## Lesson outline
1. `de-b1-u04-l1-tasks` **"Tasks at Work"**: `Was ist deine Aufgabe?`, `Die Arbeit ist
   fertig.`, `Um zehn haben wir eine Besprechung.`, `Meine Schicht beginnt um sechs.`, `Kannst
   du das heute erledigen?`, `Wir müssen das wieder machen.` `fast` is **not** allowed (not
   taught).
2. `de-b1-u04-l2-waiting-for` **"Waiting for an Answer"**: `Ich warte auf eine Antwort.`,
   `Wir freuen uns auf das Wochenende.`, `Ich freue mich über das Geschenk.`, `Ich denke oft
   an meine Familie.`, `Wir sprechen über den Plan.`, `Wer kümmert sich um die Gäste?`, `Ich
   interessiere mich für Computer.`, `Nimmst du an der Besprechung teil?` `die Kunden` is
   **not** allowed before U7 (n-noun); use `die Gäste`.
3. `de-b1-u04-l3-what-about` **"What Are You Talking About?"**: `Worauf wartest du? – Auf den
   Bus.`, `Ich warte darauf.`, `Worüber sprecht ihr?`, `Woran denkst du?`, `Wofür
   interessierst du dich?`, `Ich freue mich darauf.`, `Auf wen wartest du? – Auf meine
   Chefin.` `Auf Herrn …` is **not** allowed before U7 (n-declension).
4. `de-b1-u04-l4-printer-problem` **"The Printer Doesn't Work"**: `Der Drucker druckt
   nicht.`, `Das Programm funktioniert schlecht.`, `Ich habe einen Fehler gemacht.`, `Das ist
   falsch.`, `Eigentlich ist das ganz einfach.`, `Wer kümmert sich darum?`
5. `de-b1-u04-l5-coffee-machine` **"Scenario: At the Coffee Machine"**: two colleagues (du)
   on a Friday afternoon: `Hast du schon Feierabend?`, `Natürlich!`, `Worauf freust du dich am
   Wochenende?`, `Die Kaffeemaschine ist kaputt.`, `Schönes Wochenende!`

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-b1-u04`, title "Everyday Work", description "Talk about tasks and problems at
   work, and use verbs with prepositions, worauf and darauf.", in level B1 after
   `de-b1-u03`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-b1-u04-l1-tasks` "Tasks at Work"
   2. `de-b1-u04-l2-waiting-for` "Waiting for an Answer"
   3. `de-b1-u04-l3-what-about` "What Are You Talking About?"
   4. `de-b1-u04-l4-printer-problem` "The Printer Doesn't Work"
   5. `de-b1-u04-l5-coffee-machine` "Scenario: At the Coffee Machine"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-28** pass.
3. Each of the **8 verb + preposition items** appears in at least 2 exercises. At least 1
   MC has wrong-preposition distractors (`Ich warte für eine Antwort.`, `Ich warte an eine
   Antwort.`) and 1 has a wrong-case distractor (`Nimmst du an die Besprechung teil?`).
4. **`wo-` compounds** appear in at least 5 exercises and **`da-` compounds** in at least 5,
   across L3–L5. At least 1 MC for "Who are you waiting for?" offers `Auf wen wartest du?`
   with the distractors `Worauf wartest du?` and `Wen wartest du?`.
5. `da-` and `wo-` compounds refer only to things; for people the content uses preposition +
   pronoun (`auf ihn`, `an sie`) in at least 2 exercises. `damit`, `dabei`, `davon` are not
   used (`damit` is U8).
6. Reflexive verbs with prepositions keep the PD-053 pronoun position (`Ich freue mich sehr
   darauf.`; `Ich freue sehr mich darauf` is never accepted).
7. Every gap word of this unit (`die Arbeit`, `fertig`, `wieder`, `schlecht`, `falsch`,
   `eigentlich`, `ganz`, `natürlich`) appears in at least 2 exercises.
8. **Recycling (CDS-25):** U3 relative clauses in at least 2 exercises (`der Kollege, der …`);
   U1 polite chunks in at least 2 (requests to colleagues).
9. No employment-law topics (overtime rules, sick notes, contracts), no company or product
   brands (CDS-26).

## Vocabulary appendix
| # | Lesson | New items (de → en) | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tasks at Work | die Arbeit (work / the job), die Aufgabe (the task), die Besprechung (the meeting), fertig (finished / ready), wieder (again), erledigen (to get done / deal with), die Schicht (the shift) | 7 |
| 2 | Waiting for an Answer | *pattern:* verb + fixed preposition (+ case); warten auf (+ acc.: to wait for), sich freuen auf (+ acc.: to look forward to), sich freuen über (+ acc.: to be pleased about), denken an (+ acc.: to think of / about), sprechen über (+ acc.: to talk about), sich kümmern um (+ acc.: to take care of / see to), sich interessieren für (+ acc.: to be interested in), teilnehmen an (+ dat.: to take part in) | 8 |
| 3 | What Are You Talking About? | *pattern:* wo- / da- + preposition (wor- / dar- before a vowel); worauf (what … for / on?), woran (what … of / about?), worüber (what … about?), wofür (what … for?), darauf (for it / on it), daran (of it / about it), darüber (about it), dafür (for it) | 8 |
| 4 | The Printer Doesn't Work | schlecht (bad / badly), falsch (wrong), der Fehler (the mistake / error), der Drucker (the printer), drucken (to print), das Programm (the program / software), eigentlich (actually), ganz (quite / really: ganz einfach) | 8 |
| 5 | At the Coffee Machine | die Kaffeemaschine (the coffee machine), der Feierabend (the end of the working day), natürlich (of course / naturally), schönes Wochenende! (have a nice weekend!) | 4 |

Unit total: 35 items. `der Computer` (A2 U13), `die Antwort` (A2 U12), `der Plan` (A2 U14),
`der Chef / die Chefin`, `das Team` (A2 U11), `das Geschenk` (A2 U4) are taught. `teilnehmen`
joins at the end in subordinate clauses like any separable verb.

**Not yet taught (don't use in this unit):** `damit` (U8), `der Kunde`, `der Patient`,
non-nominative singular forms of n-nouns (U7), `das Projekt`, `fast`, `leer`, zu +
infinitive (`Ich freue mich darauf, … zu …` is U6 recycling, after U5), `sich beschweren über`
(U8).

## Out of scope
- `da-` compound + `dass` / zu-infinitive clause (recycled in U6 after U5)
- Workplace rights, contracts and pay (CDS-26; A2 U11 covered job search)

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "Some verbs need a fixed preposition: warten auf, denken an, sich freuen
  auf. Learn them as one item."; "For things, ask with wo- and answer with da-: Worauf
  wartest du? Ich warte darauf. Before a vowel: wor-, dar-."
- L3 match pairs pair the question word with its answer form (`worüber` ↔ `about what?`), not
  with each other.
