# PLT-005: Install prompt and app-update notification
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** PLT-002, CTO-004 · **Milestone:** v0.1.0

## User story
As a learner, I want a friendly invitation to install the app and a notice when a new
version is ready so that I can open Polyglow from my home screen and always have the
latest version (US-PLT-13).

## Acceptance criteria
1. The app listens for `beforeinstallprompt`, calls `preventDefault()` and keeps the event
   for later, so the browser's own mini-infobar doesn't appear.
2. When the event is available, onboarding is complete and the learner is on the Learn tab,
   a card "Install Polyglow" appears with body "Open it from your home screen, even
   offline." and two buttons: "Install" and "Not now".
3. "Install" opens the browser's native install prompt. If the learner accepts, the card
   disappears and doesn't return.
4. "Not now" hides the card and it doesn't reappear for 7 days (dismissal time stored on
   the device).
5. The card never shows when the app already runs installed
   (`display-mode: standalone`, or `navigator.standalone` on iOS).
6. On iOS Safari (no `beforeinstallprompt`), the same card instead shows the steps "Tap
   Share, then Add to Home Screen", with a "Got it" button. Same 7-day rule.
7. When a new service worker is waiting, a toast "A new version of Polyglow is ready"
   appears with a "Refresh" button. "Refresh" activates the new worker and reloads the
   page once.
8. The update toast is never shown on `/lesson/*` routes. If an update arrives during a
   lesson, the toast appears after the learner leaves the lesson.
9. The card and the toast are keyboard-operable and announced politely (`role="status"`).
10. Unit tests cover the 7-day dismissal rule (shown, dismissed, hidden on day 6, shown on
    day 8) and the "already installed" check.

## Out of scope
- Showing the prompt after the first completed lesson instead of after onboarding
  (revisit in v1.0.0 with analytics data)
- Auto-update without asking

## UX notes
- **Install card:** sits at the top of the Learn page content (not over the bottom
  navigation), with a small Lumi icon. Fits at 375 px without truncating the buttons.
- **Update toast:** CTO-004 Toast, bottom of the screen above the navigation, stays until
  dismissed or refreshed.
- **Error:** if the native prompt fails or is dismissed, the card simply hides for 7 days;
  no error message.
- **Loading/empty:** not applicable.

## Parallel work
Needs PLT-002. Can run in parallel with PLT-004 and PLT-006.
