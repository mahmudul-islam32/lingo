# LES-008: Lesson results screen
**Owner:** dev-lessons · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** LES-002, GAM-002, PLT-008 (saving), CON-001 (e2e test only) · **Milestone:** v0.2.0

## User story
As a learner, I want a results screen at the end of a lesson with XP, accuracy and time so
that I feel a sense of achievement (US-LES-13, US-GAM-04, US-GAM-05).

## Acceptance criteria
1. After the last correct "Continue", the results screen shows Lumi (celebrating pose), the
   heading "Lesson complete!" and three stats: **XP earned**, **Accuracy** and **Time**.
2. XP comes from `calculateXp` (GAM-002), never from numbers inside the lessons feature.
3. Accuracy = first-try correct ÷ counted exercises × 100, rounded to the nearest whole
   number, shown as "N %" (PD-012).
4. Time is shown as `m:ss` (for example "3:07").
5. A perfect lesson (no mistakes) also shows the line "Perfect lesson! +5 bonus XP", with
   the bonus value taken from the `calculateXp` breakdown.
6. The lesson result is saved through the contracts data-layer function (implemented by
   PLT-008) exactly once per completed lesson, even if the screen re-renders.
7. **Error state:** if saving fails, the stats still show, and a message "We couldn't save
   this lesson on your device." appears with `role="alert"` and a "Try again" button.
8. A single primary "Continue" button returns to Learn (`/`). Focus is on the heading when
   the screen opens.
9. Stats are a list with text labels, so a screen reader reads "XP earned, 15" etc.
10. At 375 × 667, the whole screen (Lumi, heading, stats, button) is visible without
    scrolling.
11. If the XP number counts up, the animation is at most 800 ms and is removed under
    `prefers-reduced-motion` (final value shown at once).
12. Playwright e2e (with CON-001 content, keyboard only): a new guest finishes onboarding,
    plays Lesson 1 with all answers correct, and sees "15" XP and "100 %". A second test
    makes one mistake, sees the exercise again at the end, and finishes with "10" XP.

## Out of scope
- Streak and daily-goal rows and celebrations (LES-010, v0.3.0)
- "Save your progress" sign-up prompt (PLT-013, v0.4.0)
- Sharing results (Phase 2)
- Review of mistakes list (Phase 2)

## UX notes
- **Success:** Lumi celebrating at top, heading, three stat cards in a row (stacked on very
  narrow screens), amber "Continue" button pinned at the bottom.
- **Loading:** saving runs in the background; no spinner blocks the stats.
- **Error:** alert under the stats; "Continue" still works (result kept in memory for retry
  until the learner leaves).
- **Empty:** not applicable (screen only shows after a finished lesson).
