## User story
As a learner, I want to tell people where I went on holiday, how I got there and how long I
stayed, so that I can share my trips and ask about theirs.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** holidays, trips, the sea, lakes and mountains, a postcard
- **Life in DACH:** a week at an Austrian lake, a city trip by train, a hike, a postcard to
  grandma
- **Can-do:** I can say where I went on holiday, how I travelled, how long I stayed and what
  I did, and say who something is for.
- **Grammar:** **Perfekt with `sein`** (movement and change); choosing `haben` or `sein`;
  **accusative personal pronouns** `mich`, `dich`, `ihn`, `uns`, `euch` (`sie`, `es`, `Sie`
  as in the nominative); **accusative prepositions** `für`, `ohne`, `durch`, `um`.

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u03-l1-on-holiday` **"On Holiday"**: `Urlaub machen`, `die Reise`, `das Meer` and
   the chunk `am Meer`, `die Berge`, `der See`, `das Hotel`, `der Koffer`. Present tense and
   `war` (`Der See war schön.`). Places: DACH cities and taught countries only.
2. `de-a2-u03-l2-we-travelled` **"We Went by Train"**: `Ich bin nach Wien gefahren.`,
   `geflogen`, `das Flugzeug`, `der Flughafen`, `gegangen`, `gekommen`, `angekommen`
   (`Wir sind um acht angekommen.`). `mit dem Zug / mit dem Flugzeug` as A1 chunk pattern.
3. `de-a2-u03-l3-how-long` **"How Long Did You Stay?"**: `Wie lange bist du geblieben? – Eine
   Woche.`, `für zwei Nächte`, `aufgestanden`, `gewandert`, `gelaufen`, `Was ist passiert?`.
   `haben` and `sein` verbs mixed in one lesson.
4. `de-a2-u03-l4-for-you` **"For You"**: `Das ist für dich.`, `für mich`, `für ihn`, `für
   uns`, `für euch`, `ohne mich`, `durch die Stadt`, `um den See`; pronouns as direct
   objects (`Ich besuche dich.`, `Wir treffen ihn morgen.`).
5. `de-a2-u03-l5-postcard` **"Scenario: A Postcard from the Alps"**: a learner writes to
   grandma: `Liebe Oma, …`, `Lieber Opa, …`, `die Aussicht`, `Ich schicke dir …` is **not**
   allowed (dative `dir` is U4); use `Ich schreibe eine Postkarte.`, `Viele Grüße`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u03`, title "Holiday Stories", description "Tell where you went, how you
   travelled and how long you stayed, and say who something is for.", in level A2 after
   `de-a2-u02`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u03-l1-on-holiday` "On Holiday"
   2. `de-a2-u03-l2-we-travelled` "We Went by Train"
   3. `de-a2-u03-l3-how-long` "How Long Did You Stay?"
   4. `de-a2-u03-l4-for-you` "For You"
   5. `de-a2-u03-l5-postcard` "Scenario: A Postcard from the Alps"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. The Perfekt with `sein` appears in at least 8 word banks across L2–L5, and every `sein`
   participle in the appendix is used at least once.
4. At least 3 multiple choices make the learner choose `haben` or `sein` (`Ich habe nach
   Wien gefahren.` as a distractor), and at least 1 word bank has both `habe` and `bin` tiles.
5. Each of `mich`, `dich`, `ihn`, `uns`, `euch` is used at least once; `für` + pronoun in at
   least 3 exercises; `ohne` in at least 2; `durch` and `um` (around) at least 1 each with an
   accusative article (`um den See`).
6. `um` = "around" is introduced with a place and never in a sentence where it could be read
   as the A1 `um` + time; the L4 tip points out both meanings.
7. L5 uses `Liebe …` with a female and `Lieber …` with a male addressee at least once each,
   and `Viele Grüße` at least twice.
8. Places are DACH cities and countries taught in A1; no other country names (CDS-3).

## Out of scope
- Dative pronouns (`dir`, `mir`, U4) and dative prepositions beyond A1 chunks (U5)
- `in den Bergen`, `im Hotel` (U9); booking a hotel (U10)
- `gegen` (B1 for "against"; time "gegen acht" is not taught)
- Countries outside DACH and A1

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "Movement verbs use sein: Ich bin nach Graz gefahren."; "für, ohne, durch,
  um take the accusative: für dich, um den See."; "um + time = at; um + place = around."
- Postcard exercises can show the greeting and closing on separate lines in the prompt; the
  answer is one sentence per word bank.

### Decisions and uncertainties (needs native review)
1. Marked but grammatical orders accepted because the tiles can't prevent them (PD-043):
   L1 ex04 `Urlaub machen wir in Österreich.`, L3 ex06 `Ein Zimmer brauche ich für zwei
   Nächte.`, L4 ex03 `Dich besuche ich morgen.`, L5 ex04 `Wir sind in Innsbruck am Montag
   angekommen.` (place before time).
2. L1 ex04 prompt "We're on holiday in Austria." for `Wir machen Urlaub in Österreich.`
   (English has no exact equivalent of `Urlaub machen`).
3. L1 ex02 avoids `die See` as a distractor (it is a real word, "the sea"); `das See` and
   `das Meer` are used instead.
4. `zum Flughafen` (L2 ex09) reuses the A1 chunk `Wie komme ich zum …?`; no free `zum` +
   noun elsewhere.
5. `für zwei Nächte` is used with `brauchen` (a booking need), not with `geblieben`, because
   `Ich bin für zwei Nächte geblieben` is less idiomatic than `zwei Nächte geblieben`.
6. Not used, to stay inside the appendices: `in den Bergen`, `am See`, `nach Hause`, `die
   Alpen`, `die Stunde`, `lang`. The L5 title mentions the Alps; the content says `die
   Berge` and Innsbruck. No vocabulary request is raised.
