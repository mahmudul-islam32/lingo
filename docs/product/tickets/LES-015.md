# LES-015: Cloze (fill-the-gap) exercise component
**Owner:** dev-lessons · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** CTO-009, LES-002, LES-003,
LES-014 · **Milestone:** v0.5.0 (non-blocking: A2 ships without it, PD-049) ·
**Decisions:** PD-012, PD-019, PD-049

## User story
As a German learner, I want to tap the right word into the gap of a German sentence, so that
I can practise articles, endings and prepositions in a few seconds per item.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The exercise shows the German `sentence` with each gap as an empty slot, the English
   `translation` under it, and the options of the **active gap** as buttons below.
2. Tapping an option fills the active gap; the next empty gap becomes active. Tapping a filled
   gap makes it active again and clears it.
3. "Check" is disabled until every gap is filled; checking uses the CTO-009 answer check.
4. **Correct:** the feedback panel (LES-003) shows the full sentence. **Incorrect:** each
   wrong gap is marked, and the panel shows the full correct sentence (`answers[0]`-style,
   authored case, `lang="de"`).
5. Scoring follows PD-012: one wrong check = 1 mistake, 1 heart (LES-009), re-queued.
6. Keyboard only: Tab moves between gaps and options, Enter / Space selects, and Enter on
   "Check" checks (FIX-001 behaviour).
7. Screen readers read the sentence with "blank 1 of 2" for each gap, the options with
   `lang` from `learningLanguage`, and the translation with `knownLanguage`.
8. At 375 × 667, a sentence of 80 characters with 2 gaps and 4 options of 24 characters fits
   without horizontal scrolling (LES-014 wrapping rules).
9. If `audio` is present, a play button reads the full sentence (PD-034 fallback).
10. Unit / RTL tests for criteria 1–7, and no serious or critical axe violations with a German
    fixture.
11. The component is registered in the exercise registry, so lessons with `cloze` play in the
    real player; an e2e test plays a fixture lesson with one cloze exercise.

## Out of scope
- Typed gaps (Phase 2)
- Adding cloze exercises to A2 content (planned follow-up content ticket once this merges)

## UX notes
- **Empty:** gaps shown as underlined slots of equal width; first gap active (amber focus
  ring).
- **Filled:** chosen word inside the slot; options for the next gap appear.
- **Loading / error:** unchanged from the lesson player (LES-002).
- **Success / failure:** feedback panel as for other types; wrong gaps outlined with the
  incorrect colour and an icon (not colour alone).
