# CON-DE-A2-06: A2 Unit 6 "Clothes and Shopping": 5 original lessons (en → de)
**Owner:** pm (content writer) · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** CON-DE-A1-QA; appendices
of CON-DE-A2-01 … 05 (PD-052) · **Milestone:** v0.5.0 · **Wave:** 2 · **Curriculum:**
`curriculum-german.md` §4b Unit 6 · **Decisions:** PD-045 … PD-052 (PD-049 for the drill)

## User story
As a learner, I want to describe clothes, ask for another size and exchange something, so
that I can shop for clothes on my own.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** clothes, colours, sizes, the fitting room, sales and exchanges
- **Life in DACH:** a department store fitting room; exchanging a jacket with the receipt
- **Can-do:** I can describe clothes by colour and size, ask for another size, say what fits
  and suits me, and exchange an item.
- **Grammar:** **basic adjective endings**, nominative and accusative only, after `der / die
  / das` and after `ein / kein / mein`; `welcher?` and `dieser`; `passen` and `stehen` +
  dative.

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u06-l1-clothes` **"Clothes"**: clothing nouns with articles and plurals (`die
   Hosen`, `die Schuhe`), `Ich brauche eine Jacke.` (A1 accusative), `Der Rock ist zu kurz` is
   **not** allowed yet (`kurz` is L4).
2. `de-a2-u06-l2-colours` **"Colours"**: `Welche Farbe hat die Jacke? – Sie ist blau.`, seven
   colours, predicative only (`Das Hemd ist weiß.`).
3. `de-a2-u06-l3-the-blue-jumper` **"The Blue Jumper"**: `der blaue Pullover`, `Ich suche den
   blauen Pullover.`, `die rote Jacke`, `das weiße Hemd`, `die schwarzen Schuhe`, `Welcher
   Mantel?`, `Dieses T-Shirt ist schön.`.
4. `de-a2-u06-l4-a-red-dress` **"A Red Dress"**: `ein blauer Pullover`, `Ich suche einen
   blauen Pullover.`, `ein rotes Kleid`, `eine weiße Jacke`, `kein schwarzer Mantel`, `Welche
   Größe haben Sie?`, `Kann ich das anprobieren?`, `Die Hose passt mir nicht, sie ist zu eng.`
5. `de-a2-u06-l5-exchange` **"Scenario: Exchanging a Jacket"**: a customer (Sie) brings back
   a jacket that is broken, a friend (du) says another one suits her: `Das steht dir gut.`,
   `umtauschen`, `zurückgeben`, `im Angebot`, `kaputt`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u06`, title "Clothes and Shopping", description "Describe clothes by colour
   and size, try things on and exchange them: adjective endings.", in level A2 after
   `de-a2-u05`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u06-l1-clothes` "Clothes"
   2. `de-a2-u06-l2-colours` "Colours"
   3. `de-a2-u06-l3-the-blue-jumper` "The Blue Jumper"
   4. `de-a2-u06-l4-a-red-dress` "A Red Dress"
   5. `de-a2-u06-l5-exchange` "Scenario: Exchanging a Jacket"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. Attributive adjectives appear in at least 10 exercises across L3–L5: after `der / die /
   das` (L3) and after `ein / kein / mein` (L4), in the nominative and accusative only, with
   at least 2 exercises per gender and 1 plural after `die`.
4. At least 4 multiple choices have options that differ **only** in the adjective ending
   (`einen blauen / blaue / blauer / blaues Pullover`), and at least 2 word banks offer two
   endings of the same adjective as tiles (CDS-14).
5. **Not used:** adjective endings in the dative (`mit dem blauen …`), adjectives without an
   article (`blaue Schuhe`), and colours that don't decline (`lila`, `rosa`).
6. `passen` and `stehen` + dative appear at least 2 times each; `gefallen` (U4) at least once.
7. `welcher / welche / welches` and `dieser / diese / dieses` appear at least 2 times each.
8. Sizes are written in words (`Größe achtunddreißig`).

Unit total: 35 items. After L3 and L4, endings of every taught adjective are allowed extras
(CDS-16), nominative and accusative only.

## Out of scope
- Dative adjective endings and adjectives without an article (B1)
- Comparatives (`billiger`, U13)
- Brand names, real stores, real prices

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "After der, die, das: -e, and -en for masculine objects: den blauen
  Pullover."; "After ein: ein blauer Pullover, ein rotes Kleid, eine weiße Jacke.";
  "passen and stehen take the dative: Die Hose passt mir."
- This unit is where a cloze drill would help most (CTO-009 / LES-015). Until it exists, the
  MC rule in criterion 4 carries the drill (PD-049).
