# GAM-005: Hearts state, lose-hearts mutation and hearts meter
**Owner:** dev-gamification · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** GAM-002, PLT-003, PLT-008, CTO-004 · **Milestone:** v0.3.0

## User story
As a learner, I want to always know how many hearts I have and when the next one comes
back, and to lose a heart when I answer wrongly, so that I understand the stakes
(US-GAM-10, US-GAM-11, US-GAM-12).

## Context
The pure hearts rules (`createFullHearts`, `loseHearts`, `refillHearts`, `viewHearts`:
lose 1 per mistake, refill 1 per 4 h, max 5) were shipped by GAM-002, and
`ProgressRepository.getHearts` / `saveHearts` exist in `@/lib/offline` (PLT-003). This
ticket wires them for the UI: a read hook, a write mutation, a countdown formatter and a
small presentational meter that the top bar (GAM-007) and the lesson player (LES-009)
both render.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A hook `useHearts()` is exported from `@/features/gamification` and returns
   `{ status, view }`, where `view` is a `HeartsView` = `viewHearts(stored, now)`. It reads
   through the `@/lib/offline` data layer only.
2. While the app is open, `view.current` goes up at `view.nextRefillAt` without a reload
   or user action. The hook does not re-render more than once per minute while waiting
   (no per-second polling).
3. When the page becomes visible again (`visibilitychange`), the view is recomputed at the
   current time.
4. A mutation hook `useLoseHearts()` takes a `cost` (a non-negative integer, from
   `AnswerCheckResult.heartCost`), applies `loseHearts(latestStored, cost, now)` and
   persists the result with `saveHearts` straight away (so quitting a lesson does not undo
   the loss, ARCHITECTURE §3).
5. The `["hearts"]` query data updates before the save finishes, so a meter showing it
   changes within 100 ms of the call.
6. Two `useLoseHearts` calls made one right after the other both apply (5 → 3), never
   overwrite each other.
7. A cost of 0 does not write anything. Hearts never go below 0.
8. If saving fails, the in-memory hearts still go down for the rest of the app session,
   the mutation reports the error to the caller, and nothing crashes.
9. A pure function `formatHeartRefill(view, now)` returns:
   - `null` when hearts are full;
   - "Next heart in less than a minute" when under 60 s remain;
   - "Next heart in N min" under 1 hour (minutes rounded up, never "0 min");
   - "Next heart in H h M min" from 1 hour (e.g. "Next heart in 3 h 12 min"; "3 h" when
     M is 0).
10. A pure function `formatHeartsFull(view, now)` returns "Full in H h M min" (same
    rounding) or `null` when full.
11. A presentational component `HeartsMeter` (props: `current`, `max`, optional `size`)
    shows the `HeartIcon` (CTO-004) and the number. The icon is filled when `current > 0`
    and outlined at 0. Its accessible name is "Hearts: N of 5" (max from props).
12. `HeartsMeter` meets 3:1 contrast for the icon and 4.5:1 for the number in both themes
    (uses the `heart` and `fg` tokens only, no colour literals).
13. Unit tests cover: full view; after losing 1 (clock starts); refill arriving while
    mounted (fake timers); 2 rapid losses; cost 0; save failure; every
    `formatHeartRefill` / `formatHeartsFull` branch (59 s, 60 s, 59 min 1 s, 1 h, 3 h 12 min);
    `HeartsMeter` accessible name at 5 and 0.

## UX notes
- **Meter:** heart icon + bold number, e.g. "♥ 4" (icon is the CTO-004 `HeartIcon`).
  Compact enough for a 375 px top bar and the lesson header.
- **Loading:** consumers show a skeleton the size of the meter (no layout shift).
- **Error:** if hearts can't be read, consumers treat the learner as having full hearts
  (`createFullHearts`) so nobody is blocked by a storage problem; log the error.
- **Empty:** not applicable (a learner always has 0–5 hearts).
- **0 hearts:** outlined icon and "0"; the countdown text from criterion 9 is shown by
  the consumer.
