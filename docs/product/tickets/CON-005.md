# CON-005: "Life in Germany" topics: map topics to existing A1 and A2 lessons
**Owner:** pm · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** CTO-023 (data shape; the mapping review in
criteria 1–5 can start at once) · **Milestone:** v0.5.6 Life in Germany topics ·
**Decisions:** PD-064, PD-071 #5, PD-086

## User story
As a newcomer to Germany, I want to find the lessons for a situation I'm facing this week
(the registration office, the doctor, the train) so that I can practise exactly what I need
(US-DE-01, US-TP-01).

## Background
The topic index uses **only existing `en-de` lessons**: no new lessons, exercises, tips or
sentences, and no lesson file is edited. The pm decides which lessons belong to which topic
and writes them in the shape CTO-023 defines. The draft mapping below comes from the lesson
titles and a keyword scan of the lesson files (2026-11-28); criteria 2–4 confirm it lesson by
lesson.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Exactly **7 topics** for `en-de`, in this order: registering your address, at the doctor's,
   at the supermarket, bus and train, your flat, work, letters and forms. Topic ids are slugs
   (as in the draft table).
2. **Inclusion rule**, checked for every listed lesson: the lesson's situation is the topic
   (its scenario, title or tip names it), **or** at least half of its graded exercises use the
   topic's situation or vocabulary. Each row of the final table gives a reason of at most 15
   words (e.g. "Scenario at the Bürgeramt; 6 of 10 exercises on forms").
3. Every topic has **3–12 lessons**, listed in course (path) order. Level checkpoints
   (`de-a1-u12-l5-a1-checkpoint`, `de-a2-u14-l5-a2-checkpoint`) are never listed: they mix
   every topic.
4. A lesson appears in **at most 2 topics**. Every lesson id exists in
   `content/courses/en-de/course.json` (the CTO-023 content test enforces it).
5. Titles at most 30 characters and descriptions at most 90 characters (or the CTO-023 limits
   if smaller), in English, original, kind, in the second person. No exam names, no "test",
   "pass" or "certificate" (PD-035), no official forms reproduced, safe topics only (PD-051).
6. The data file is written in the shape and location CTO-023 decides and passes
   `npm run test` (content test) and `npm run lint` (Prettier). No other file in `/content`
   changes. If the topics live in `course.json`, `course.version` is bumped once.
7. The final table (criteria 1–5) replaces the draft table in this ticket, so GAM-016's
   reviewer can check the screen against it.
8. Other courses (`telc-b1-en-de`, `einbuergerung-en-de`, `en-es`) get **no** topics.
