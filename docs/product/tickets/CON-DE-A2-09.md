# CON-DE-A2-09: A2 Unit 9 "At Home and the Neighbours": 5 original lessons (en → de)
**Owner:** pm (content writer) · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** CON-DE-A1-QA; appendices
of CON-DE-A2-01 … 08 (PD-052) · **Milestone:** v0.5.0 · **Wave:** 3 · **Curriculum:**
`curriculum-german.md` §4b Unit 9 · **Decisions:** PD-040, PD-045 … PD-052 (PD-049 for the
drill)

## User story
As a learner who has just moved in, I want to say where things are and where I put them,
talk to my neighbours and report a repair, so that I feel at home in my building.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** arranging a flat, location and direction, neighbours, house rules, rubbish,
  repairs
- **Life in DACH:** separating rubbish into bins, the building's house rules on a notice
  board, calling the caretaker
- **Can-do:** I can say where things are and where I put them, talk politely with
  neighbours, understand simple house rules and report something broken.
- **Grammar:** **two-way prepositions** `in, an, auf, unter, über, neben, vor, hinter,
  zwischen`: **Wo? + dative** vs **Wohin? + accusative**; `liegen / stehen / hängen` vs
  `legen / stellen / hängen`; `im / ins`, `am / ans`; productive `in den / in die / ins`
  (completes PD-040).

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u09-l1-where-is-it` **"Where Is It?"**: `Das Buch liegt auf dem Tisch.`, `Die
   Katze ist unter dem Bett.`, `Die Lampe steht neben dem Sofa.`, `Das Foto ist an der
   Wand.`, `über dem Tisch`, `im Schrank`. Rooms and furniture from A1 U10.
2. `de-a2-u09-l2-between` **"In Front, Behind, Between"**: `vor der Tür`, `hinter dem
   Haus` is **not** allowed (`das Haus` is L4); use `hinter dem Sofa`, `zwischen dem Bett
   und dem Schrank`, `Das Bild hängt an der Wand.`, `der Teppich`, `in der Ecke`.
3. `de-a2-u09-l3-where-does-it-go` **"Where Does It Go?"**: `Ich lege das Buch auf den
   Tisch.`, `Wir stellen das Sofa ins Wohnzimmer.`, `Ich hänge das Bild an die Wand.`, `in
   den Keller`, `in die Küche`, `die Kiste`. The tip links to the A1 chunk `ins Kino`.
4. `de-a2-u09-l4-neighbours` **"Good Neighbours"**: `Hallo, ich bin die neue Nachbarin.`,
   `Der Müll kommt in die Mülltonne.`, `Wir trennen den Müll.`, `Bitte seien Sie abends
   leise.` is **not** allowed (formal imperative of `sein` not taught); use `Bitte sprechen
   Sie abends leise.`, `Störe ich?`, `die Hausordnung`.
5. `de-a2-u09-l5-repairs` **"Scenario: The Tap Is Dripping"**: the learner calls the
   caretaker (Sie): the heating doesn't work, something drips in the bathroom, the key is
   lost: `der Hausmeister / die Hausmeisterin`, `reparieren`, `funktionieren`, `der
   Schlüssel`, `tropfen`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u09`, title "At Home and the Neighbours", description "Say where things are
   and where they go, meet your neighbours and report a repair.", in level A2 after
   `de-a2-u08`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u09-l1-where-is-it` "Where Is It?"
   2. `de-a2-u09-l2-between` "In Front, Behind, Between"
   3. `de-a2-u09-l3-where-does-it-go` "Where Does It Go?"
   4. `de-a2-u09-l4-neighbours` "Good Neighbours"
   5. `de-a2-u09-l5-repairs` "Scenario: The Tap Is Dripping"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. Two-way prepositions appear in at least 12 exercises; each of the 9 prepositions at least
   once; both cases at least 4 times each.
4. At least 4 multiple choices contrast dative and accusative in otherwise equal sentences
   (`liegt auf dem Tisch` / `liegt auf den Tisch`), and every `Wo?` / `Wohin?` prompt pair
   is clear from the English ("is on" vs "am putting … on").
5. `liegen / legen` and `stehen / stellen` each appear in at least 2 multiple choices as a
   contrast.
6. `ins`, `in den`, `in die` appear in at least 4 exercises; `im` with a noun (not a month)
   appears for the first time in A2 here.
7. House rules are the building's own rules (`In unserem Haus …`). No statements about laws,
   quiet-hour regulations, fines or tenancy rights (PD-051).
8. The L5 caretaker call uses `Sie`; at least 2 problem reports use the Perfekt or `konnte`
   (`Ich habe meinen Schlüssel vergessen.`).

## Vocabulary appendix
| # | Lesson | New items (de → en) | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where Is It? | *pattern:* Wo? + dative; auf (on), unter (under), neben (next to), an / am (on / at, a wall or edge: an der Wand, am Fenster), über (above / over), liegen (to lie, be lying), stehen (to stand, be standing), die Wand (the wall) | 8 |
| 2 | In Front, Behind, Between | vor (in front of), hinter (behind), zwischen (between), hängen (to hang, be hanging), das Bild (the picture), der Teppich (the rug / carpet), die Tür (the door), die Ecke (the corner) | 8 |
| 3 | Where Does It Go? | *pattern:* Wohin? + accusative; stellen (to put, upright), legen (to put, flat / lay), ins (into the: in das), in den / in die (into the, + accusative), der Keller (the cellar / basement), die Kiste (the box / crate) | 6 |
| 4 | Good Neighbours | der Nachbar / die Nachbarin (the neighbour), das Haus (the house / building), der Müll (rubbish / trash), die Mülltonne (the bin), trennen (to separate / sort), leise (quiet / quietly), stören (to disturb), die Hausordnung (the house rules) | 8 |
| 5 | The Tap Is Dripping | der Hausmeister / die Hausmeisterin (the caretaker / janitor), reparieren (to repair), funktionieren (to work, of a machine), der Schlüssel (the key), tropfen (to drip) | 5 |

Unit total: 35 items. `hängen` with the accusative (direction) is a use of the L2 item, not
a new one. `kaputt` (U6) and the A1 flat nouns are recycled. `der Wasserhahn` (tap) is not
taught; the English title says "tap", the German says `Im Bad tropft es.`.

## Out of scope
- Two-way prepositions in time expressions beyond A1 / A2 chunks (`vor einer Woche` is U5)
- `sich setzen / sitzen`, `stecken` (B1)
- Rent law, deposits, notice periods (no legal claims)

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "Wo? → dative: auf dem Tisch. Wohin? → accusative: auf den Tisch.";
  "liegen / stehen = where it is; legen / stellen = where you put it."; "ins = in das, like
  ins Kino from A1."
- A cloze drill would help here too (CTO-009 / LES-015); until then criterion 4 carries the
  drill (PD-049).
