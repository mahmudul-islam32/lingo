## User story
As a German learner on a small phone, I want every A1 unit to play without overflow, with
German audio and correct language tags, so that I can finish A1 without getting stuck.

## Problem
CON-DE-A1-QA criterion 7 was left open. Today's browser evidence covers A1 Lesson 1 only, at
390 × 844. The per-unit run at 375 × 667 with the German speech fallback hasn't been done, and
the fallback itself (de-DE preference) arrives with LES-014.

## Acceptance criteria
1. On a build of the release-candidate commit (with #55, FIX-003 and the A2 QA merged), a
   scripted Chromium run at **375 × 667** plays **one lesson per A1 unit (12 lessons)** plus
   the **A1 checkpoint** (`de-a1-u12-l5-a1-checkpoint`) to "Lesson complete!" with the correct
   answers from the content files. Every correct answer is accepted (0 "Not quite").
2. The run includes `de-a1-u03-l5-spell-it` (letter tiles). With a `de-DE` voice available, the
   speech fallback is called with a `de-DE` voice (the utterance `lang` / voice is logged), and
   the slow button uses rate 0.7.
3. The run includes the 9 listen exercises whose transcripts changed in the A1 QA:
   u03-l3-ex09, u05-l5-ex07, u05-l5-ex10, u08-l5-ex02, u10-l3-ex12, u11-l1-ex09, u11-l4-ex11,
   u12-l3-ex03, u12-l5-ex08. For each, the text sent to speech synthesis equals the transcript
   in the content file.
4. With **no** German voice available, a listen exercise shows "Audio isn't available right
   now." and Skip, and never speaks with an English or Spanish voice.
5. No screen in the run scrolls horizontally (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth <=
   innerWidth`), every tip card shows with "Start" visible, and learning-language text is
   inside a `lang="de"` element.
6. The results are recorded in the Review section below: lesson ids played, answers accepted,
   voice used, and any issue. Each issue becomes its own `fix/` ticket for its owner and
   stops the release.
