# LES-013: Lesson tip card
**Owner:** dev-lessons · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** LES-002, LES-014 ·
**Milestone:** v0.5.0 (moved from v1.0.0 by PD-050) · **Decisions:** PD-006, PD-050

## User story
As a learner, I want to see the lesson's short grammar tip before I start, so that I know the
rule I'm about to practise (US-LES-16).

## Acceptance criteria
1. When a lesson has a `tip`, a tip card is shown before the first exercise, with the tip
   text, a small Lumi (static pose, PD-016) and a "Start" button.
2. "Start" dismisses the card and shows exercise 1. The card is not an exercise: it doesn't
   move the progress bar, count in accuracy or cost hearts.
3. Lessons without a `tip` start directly with exercise 1 (no empty card).
4. Tips up to 280 characters (contract limit; A2 content uses ≤ 200, CDS-15) fit at
   375 × 667 without scrolling the "Start" button off screen.
5. The tip text has `lang` from the course's `knownLanguage` (English); German example words
   inside it are not separately tagged in this ticket.
6. Keyboard: focus starts on "Start"; Enter / Space starts the lesson. Screen readers read
   the tip before the button.
7. The quit dialog (LES-002) works from the tip card.
8. RTL tests for criteria 1–3, 6, 7; no serious or critical axe violations.

## Out of scope
- Re-opening the tip during the lesson; "don't show tips again" setting
- Tables or rich grammar notes (Phase 2 grammar note card, curriculum §6)
- `lang` tagging of German words inside tips (needs a contract markup, Phase 2)

## UX notes
- **Success:** card with the tip, Lumi idle pose, "Start".
- **Empty:** no tip → no card.
- **Loading / error:** unchanged from the lesson player.
