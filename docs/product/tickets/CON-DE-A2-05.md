## User story
As a newcomer, I want to run errands at the bank and the post office and understand large
amounts, so that I can manage money, parcels and appointments in town.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** going to places, being at places, bank, cash machine, post office, numbers in
  the hundreds and thousands, years
- **Life in DACH:** a Saturday of errands; opening a bank account after moving
- **Can-do:** I can say where I am going and where I am, ask the way, open a bank account,
  send a parcel, and understand amounts and years in the hundreds and thousands.
- **Grammar:** **dative prepositions** `zu`, `bei`, `von`, `mit`, `nach`, `aus`, `seit`,
  `gegenüber (von)`; `zum`, `zur`, `beim`, `vom`; `nach Hause` vs `zu Hause`; `seit` and
  `vor` + dative for time; **numbers 100 – 1,000,000 and years** (PD-041).

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u05-l1-going-to` **"To the Bank, to the Post Office"**: `Ich gehe zur Bank.`,
   `zum Supermarkt`, `zur Post`, `zum Arzt` (A1 noun), `nach Hause`. The tip explains the A1
   chunk `Wie komme ich zum Bahnhof?` (`zum` = `zu dem`).
2. `de-a2-u05-l2-where-are-you` **"Where Are You?"**: `Ich bin beim Arzt / bei der Bank / bei
   meiner Schwester.`, `Ich komme vom Bahnhof.`, `mit meinem Bruder`, `Gehen Sie nach links.`
   (resolves the A1 native-review note on `nach links / rechts`), `gegenüber vom Supermarkt`.
3. `de-a2-u05-l3-hundreds` **"Hundreds and Thousands"**: `zweihundert`, `dreihundertfünfzig`,
   `tausend`, `zweitausend`, `eine Million`, years (`neunzehnhundertneunzig`,
   `zweitausendzwanzig`), `Ich möchte zweihundert Euro abheben.`, `der Geldautomat`.
4. `de-a2-u05-l4-since-when` **"Since When?"**: `Seit wann wohnen Sie hier? – Seit einem
   Jahr.` (present tense!), `vor zwei Jahren`, `Ich möchte ein Konto eröffnen.`, `der
   Ausweis`, `die Bankkarte`. Formal register at the bank.
5. `de-a2-u05-l5-post-office` **"Scenario: At the Post Office"**: send a parcel to India and
   a letter in Germany, buy stamps, pay at the counter: `das Paket`, `der Brief`, `die
   Briefmarke`, `der Schalter`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u05`, title "Errands in Town", description "Go to the bank and the post
   office, say where you are and since when, and use big numbers.", in level A2 after
   `de-a2-u04`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u05-l1-going-to` "To the Bank, to the Post Office"
   2. `de-a2-u05-l2-where-are-you` "Where Are You?"
   3. `de-a2-u05-l3-hundreds` "Hundreds and Thousands"
   4. `de-a2-u05-l4-since-when` "Since When?"
   5. `de-a2-u05-l5-post-office` "Scenario: At the Post Office"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. Each of `zu`, `bei`, `von`, `mit`, `seit` appears in at least 2 exercises; `aus` and
   `nach` at least once each with the full dative where an article is needed (`aus der
   Schweiz` explained, `nach Hause`).
4. Each contraction `zum`, `zur`, `beim`, `vom` appears at least once, and 1 multiple choice
   offers `Ich gehe zur Post.` / `zum Post` / `zu die Post` (only `zur Post` is correct).
   `zu der` / `bei dem` (uncontracted) are never distractors, because they are grammatical.
5. 1 multiple choice contrasts `nach Hause` (direction) with `zu Hause` (location).
6. **Hundreds (PD-041 now productive):** at least 5 exercises use numbers 100–999, at least 2
   of them `to_learning` word banks; `tausend` in at least 2; years in at least 2 (as MC
   options when the word is longer than 24 characters, CDS-18).
7. `seit` + **present tense** appears in at least 2 exercises (`Ich wohne seit zwei Jahren
   hier.`), and 1 multiple choice has the English-style Perfekt distractor (`Ich habe seit
   zwei Jahren hier gewohnt.`).
8. No real bank names, no account fees, interest or financial advice; no customs or postage
   rules (PD-051).

## Out of scope
- `in der Bank`, `auf der Post` and other two-way prepositions (U9)
- Bank transfers, loans, contracts; customs forms
- Ordinal numbers above A1 (`hundertste`)

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "zu, bei, mit, von, seit, aus, nach always take the dative: mit dem Bus.";
  "zum = zu dem, zur = zu der, beim = bei dem, vom = von dem."; "Years: 1990 =
  neunzehnhundertneunzig; 2020 = zweitausendzwanzig."
- English prompts may use digits for amounts and years (CDS-18); tiles never do.

### Uncertainties / needs native review
1. **Marked orders are rejected.** This is a deliberate exception to PD-043, as in U4. These
   orders can all be built from the tiles: `Ich gehe zu Ana mit meinem Bruder.` (L2 ex11),
   `Ich arbeite bei der Bank seit einem Jahr.` (L4 ex12), `Ich bin nach Deutschland vor zwei
   Jahren gekommen.` (L4 ex06), `Ich möchte nach Berlin den Brief schicken.` (L5 ex06), `Ich
   bin bei der Post mit meiner Schwester.` (L5 ex12) and the echo question `Wohnen Sie seit
   wann in Deutschland?` (L4 ex04). The L4 ex12 order is common in speech, so QA may want to
   add it.
2. **`bei der Bank` / `bei der Post` for location** (L2, L4 ex12, L5 ex12) sounds natural for
   "at the bank / post office" and for working there. Some speakers prefer `in der Bank` /
   `auf der Post`, which are U9. Please confirm.
3. `gegenüber dem Supermarkt` (without `von`) is also correct standard German. I left out a
   `dem` tile so it can't be built. Only `gegenüber vom …` is taught.
4. L1 ex07: `Ist das die Straße zur Bank?` → "Is this / that the street to the bank?" "The way
   to the bank" would be more idiomatic, but `der Weg` isn't taught.
5. L3 ex09 accepts both "eight hundred and fifty" (UK) and "eight hundred fifty" (US).
6. L3 ex06: the distractor `neunzehnneunzig` copies the English "nineteen ninety". It isn't a
   German form, but it is the typical learner error.
7. L2 ex12: `Gehen Sie bitte links.` (without `nach`) is also correct, but it isn't offered.
8. L3 ex12 (a flat for a million euros, "very expensive") is a playful amount, not a market
   claim. Swap it if QA finds it off-tone.
9. L4 ex04 maps "How long have you lived …?" to `Seit wann …?` (the appendix gloss). `Wie
   lange wohnen Sie schon …?` is also natural, but there is no `lange` tile.
10. In L1, bare `zu` (without an article) appears only as a distractor tile. The first bare
    `zu` + name is in L2 ex11. Criterion 3 counts `zum` / `zur` as `zu`.
