# PLT-010: Supabase client, email sign-up and log-in, session handling
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** CTO-025 (criteria 1 and 12 need
its flag rule and local harness; the screens can be built before it merges) · **Milestone:**
v0.6.0 Accounts and sync, part A (local Supabase) · **Decisions:** PD-085, PD-087

## User story
As a learner, I want to sign up and log in with my email and a password so that my progress is
saved to an account (US-PLT-06).

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Accounts flag (ADR-020):** without `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL` / `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY`
   the app shows no sign-up or log-in entry anywhere, `/sign-up` and `/log-in` redirect to
   Learn, and an e2e in the default project asserts that no request goes to a Supabase host.
2. `/sign-up` has labelled fields Email and Password, the hint "At least 8 characters", a "Create
   account" button and a "Already have an account? Log in" link to `/log-in`.
3. Client checks on submit: an invalid email shows "Enter a valid email address."; a password
   under 8 characters shows "Use at least 8 characters." Each message is under its field, linked
   with `aria-describedby`, and focus moves to the first invalid field. Nothing is sent.
4. While submitting, the button shows "Creating your account…", is disabled and the form can't
   be sent twice.
5. Server errors use kind copy, never a raw error: email already registered → "There's already
   an account with this email." with a "Log in instead" link; rate limit → "Too many attempts.
   Please wait a minute and try again."; network → "We couldn't reach Polyglow. Check your
   connection and try again."
6. `/log-in` has Email, Password and "Log in". Wrong credentials → "That email and password
   don't match." (never says which one is wrong). A "Forgot password?" link to `/reset-password`
   is rendered **only once PLT-012 is merged** (no dead link, PD-077).
7. **Email confirmation pending** (hosted setting, PD-087): when sign-up returns no session, a
   "Check your inbox" screen says "Open the link we sent to <email> to finish creating your
   account." with "Back to Learn". The learner stays a guest meanwhile and loses nothing.
8. **Offline:** both screens show "You're offline. Connect to the internet to sign up or log in."
   and the submit button is disabled; guest learning is unaffected.
9. Password field: a "Show password" toggle button (`aria-pressed`); `autocomplete` is `email`,
   `new-password` on sign-up and `current-password` on log-in.
10. **Session:** after sign-up or log-in the session survives a reload and refreshes itself; a
    signed-in learner can still play lessons offline. A `useSession()` hook exposes
    `signed-out | signed-in | expired` (expired = refresh failed on reconnect) for PLT-014 and
    PLT-016.
11. On success the screens call `finishSignIn()` in `src/features/auth`, then go to `/` (or to a
    same-origin `next` path). In this ticket `finishSignIn()` only stores the session; the guest
    → account switch and migration are PLT-013. The routes are reachable by URL; their entry
    points are PLT-013, PLT-016 and PLT-018.
12. Tests: RTL with a mocked client for criteria 2–9; e2e in the `accounts` project against local
    Supabase: sign up a new email, reload, still signed in; log in with an existing user; wrong
    password shows the criterion 6 message.
13. At 375 × 667 no horizontal scroll, fields and buttons at least 48 px high, and the submit
    button stays reachable with the on-screen keyboard open (the form scrolls). No serious or
    critical axe violations; AA contrast. `npm run typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, `build` and
    existing e2e pass.

## Out of scope
- Google (PLT-011), password reset (PLT-012), migration and identity switch (PLT-013), log out
  (PLT-017), entry points in Profile and onboarding (PLT-016, PLT-018)
- Display name or avatar editing (US-PLT-11, P2)
- Account deletion (PLT-032, planned)

## UX notes
- **Layout:** Lumi small at the top, Bricolage heading "Create your account" / "Welcome back",
  one column, primary 3D Button full width, links in indigo under it.
- **Loading:** criterion 4. **Error:** criteria 3 and 5, inline under the field or above the
  button, announced with `role="alert"`. **Empty:** fresh form. **Success:** redirect, no toast
  (PLT-013 shows the "progress saved" moment).
