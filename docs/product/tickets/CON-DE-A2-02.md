## User story
As a learner, I want to say how my weekend was and what I did, so that I can join the
Monday small talk with friends and colleagues.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** the past weekend, yesterday evening, Monday at work
- **Life in DACH:** the office kitchen on Monday morning: "Wie war dein Wochenende?"
- **Can-do:** I can say how something was and tell someone what I did yesterday or at the
  weekend.
- **Grammar:** Präteritum `war` / `hatte` (all persons); **Perfekt with `haben`**: `haben`
  in position 2, participle at the end; regular `ge-…-t` / `ge-…-et`; irregular `gegessen`,
  `getrunken`, `gesehen`, `gelesen`, `geschlafen`, `getroffen`, `gesprochen`; separable
  (`eingekauft`), inseparable (`besucht`, `bezahlt`) and `-ieren` (`telefoniert`)
  participles. **Only `haben` verbs** in this unit.

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u02-l1-how-was-it` **"How Was It?"**: `Wie war dein Wochenende? – Es war toll /
   langweilig.`, `Ich war krank.`, `Wir hatten keine Zeit.`, `letzte Woche`, `letztes
   Wochenende`, `vorgestern`. All persons of `war` and `hatte` (`ihr wart`, `sie waren`).
2. `de-a2-u02-l2-what-did-you-do` **"What Did You Do?"**: the Perfekt frame (`Ich habe
   Fußball gespielt.`), `gemacht`, `gekauft`, `gekocht`, `gearbeitet` (`-et` after `t`),
   `gestern Abend`, `zu Hause`. Time-first inversion (`Gestern Abend habe ich gekocht.`).
3. `de-a2-u02-l3-eaten-and-seen` **"Eaten, Drunk, Seen"**: irregular participles; `sehen`
   and `schlafen` as new verbs with their stem changes (`du siehst`, `du schläfst`), `treffen`
   (`Ich habe Ana im Kino getroffen` is **not** allowed: `im` + noun is U9; use `Ich habe
   Ana am Samstag getroffen.`).
4. `de-a2-u02-l4-phoned-and-visited` **"Phoned, Visited, Paid"**: `ge` in the middle of
   separable verbs (`eingekauft`, `angerufen`, `ferngesehen`, `aufgeräumt`), no `ge` for
   `be-` verbs and `-ieren` verbs (`besucht`, `bezahlt`, `telefoniert`).
5. `de-a2-u02-l5-monday-at-work` **"Scenario: Monday at Work"**: two colleagues (du, marked
   "(to a colleague, informal)") in the office: `das Büro`, `die Pause`, `erzählen`, `nichts
   Besonderes`, `ziemlich`. Mix of `war`, `hatte` and Perfekt.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u02`, title "How Was Your Weekend?", description "Say how things were and
   what you did: the past with war, hatte and the Perfekt with haben.", in level A2 after
   `de-a2-u01`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u02-l1-how-was-it` "How Was It?"
   2. `de-a2-u02-l2-what-did-you-do` "What Did You Do?"
   3. `de-a2-u02-l3-eaten-and-seen` "Eaten, Drunk, Seen"
   4. `de-a2-u02-l4-phoned-and-visited` "Phoned, Visited, Paid"
   5. `de-a2-u02-l5-monday-at-work` "Scenario: Monday at Work"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. The Perfekt with `haben` appears in at least 8 word banks across L2–L5, with the
   participle at the end; at least 2 of them front a time phrase (`Gestern habe ich …`).
4. At least 2 multiple choices offer the participle-position error (`Ich habe gemacht
   Pizza.`) and at least 1 offers a wrong participle (`gegesst`, `geeinkauft`).
5. **No `sein` Perfekt anywhere** in this unit: no `gefahren`, `gegangen`, `gekommen`,
   `geblieben`, `aufgestanden`, `gelaufen`, `gewandert` (U3).
6. `war` and `hatte` appear in at least 5 exercises, with at least 3 different persons.
7. L4: separable participles in at least 3 exercises; `be-` / `-ieren` participles in at
   least 3.
8. English follows CDS-19: "I played football yesterday" is the accepted `to_known` answer,
   "I have played football yesterday" is not; without a time word, both "I cooked" and "I
   have cooked" are accepted when the tiles allow them.

## Out of scope
- Perfekt with `sein` (U3); Präteritum of other verbs (B1)
- Participles of modal verbs (`gekonnt`): not taught; past modals are `konnte` etc. (U8)
- `im Kino`, `im Büro` and other `in` + article phrases (U9)
