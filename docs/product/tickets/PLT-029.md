# PLT-029: `/review` route, loading the review items, saving outcomes on the device, e2e
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** CTO-019, GAM-015, LES-023,
PLT-025 · **Milestone:** v0.5.5 Smart review · **Decisions:** PD-065, PD-079, PD-080, PD-081

## User story
As a learner, I want "Start review" to open a short review straight away, also offline, and
my answers to be kept on my device so that the next review knows what I need (US-SR-02,
US-SR-04).

## Acceptance criteria
Names follow CTO-019 and its ADR.

### Storage (`src/lib/offline`)
1. A finished session's outcomes are stored with its result in **one** transaction and one
   sync record (CTO-019 criterion 3). Saving the same result twice is a no-op. Unit tests
   with the Dexie store (fake-indexeddb) and the memory fallback.
2. Outcomes are readable per course after a reload (e.g. through `listLessonResults` when
   they are embedded, or the ADR's method). Rows from before CTO-019 (no outcomes) still
   load; a corrupt row is skipped and reported as today (PLT-008 criterion 8).
3. If the ADR adds a table or index: the Dexie upgrade from version 1 keeps every existing
   result and queue record (upgrade test). Otherwise no Dexie version bump.
4. The guest migration payload and the sync queue carry the outcomes and the review kind
   unchanged (unit test on the built payload). No network call is added.

### Route `/review`
5. `/review` (inside the app shell, no bottom nav during play, like `/lesson/<id>`) reads the
   active course, calls `useReviewQueue` (GAM-015) and loads the lessons of the ranked
   candidates **in order** until it has `reviewSessionSize` items whose exercise exists in
   the loaded lesson. Candidates whose lesson or exercise no longer exists are skipped.
6. It plays the items with the review mode of the player (LES-023) and shows the review
   results screen; "Continue" goes to `/practice`; "Review more" re-reads the queue (after
   the save) and starts a new review with no page reload.
7. **Not locked by the path:** `/review` never runs the path-lock check (every item was
   already answered, PD-079). It works at 0 hearts.
8. **Empty:** with `eligibleCount = 0` (or no candidate loads), `/review` shows Lumi, "Nothing
   to review yet. Answer exercises in any lesson and they come back here." and a primary link
   "Go to Practice". No player, no result.
9. **Loading:** `LessonPlayerSkeleton` while the queue and lessons load.
10. **Error:** if the queue read fails, or every candidate lesson fails to load because of a
    network or storage error (not because it is missing), `/review` shows the lesson error
    state (`LessonPlayerError`) with "Try again" (re-reads) and "Back" (to `/practice`).
11. **Offline:** with the network off, a review whose source lessons were opened before (or
    preloaded on Learn) starts and saves; lessons that can't load offline are skipped as in
    criterion 5.
12. After a save, `["progress", courseId]` is invalidated, so Practice's card, Learn's ring
    and the top bar's XP and streak update without a reload.

### Quality
13. Accessibility: the empty and error states have a heading and focusable actions; focus
    moves to the player or the state's heading on load; no serious or critical axe
    violations on `/review` in any state.
14. At 375 × 667 the empty and error states fit without horizontal scrolling; buttons are at
    least 48 px tall.
15. RTL tests cover criteria 5–10 and 12 (mocked hooks and content).
16. e2e (Playwright, 375 px, keyboard only, fixed clock): onboard into German, finish A1
    Lesson 1 with one wrong answer → Practice shows "Review" with a due count ≥ 1 → "Start
    review" → play to the end → "Review complete!" with XP → "Continue" lands on Practice,
    and Lesson 1 is still listed once (the review is not a lesson). A second e2e: a new
    learner opens `/review` → the empty state.
17. `npm run typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, `build` and the full e2e suite pass.

## Out of scope
- The selection rule and the Practice card (GAM-015); the player and results in review mode
  (LES-023)
- Server sync of outcomes (v0.6.0, PLT-014 carries them with lesson results)
- A review deep link from Learn, notifications, a review count on the nav

## UX notes
- **States:** loading (player skeleton), empty (Lumi + "Go to Practice"), error (try again /
  back), playing, results, saved / save error (as LES-008).
- The tab bar stays hidden during play as on the lesson route; "Back" and "Continue" always
  return to Practice, where review starts.
- Copy: "review", "ready for review"; never "weak", "mistakes" or exam words (PD-035).
