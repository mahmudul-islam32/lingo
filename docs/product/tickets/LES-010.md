# LES-010: Results screen: streak and daily-goal rows with celebration
**Owner:** dev-lessons · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** LES-008, GAM-003, GAM-004 · **Milestone:** v0.3.0

## User story
As a learner, I want the results screen to show my streak and daily goal, and to
celebrate when I extend my streak or hit my goal, so that the milestone feels rewarding
(US-GAM-13, PRD §6.1 results screen).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Below the three LES-008 stats, the results screen shows two rows: **Streak** and
   **Daily goal**, each with a text label and a value.
2. Values are computed from the saved results plus the result just finished, using the
   gamification public API only: `computeStreak` / `didExtendStreak` /
   `formatStreakDays` (GAM-002, GAM-004) and `computeDailyGoalProgress` (GAM-002) with
   the learner's goal (GAM-003). No streak or goal logic is written in the lessons
   feature.
3. The rows appear at once with the stats; they don't wait for the save to finish.
4. **Streak row:**
   - first lesson ever: "Streak started!" and "1 day";
   - first lesson today (streak extended): "Streak extended!" and "N days";
   - another lesson on the same day: "Streak" and "N days", no celebration.
5. **Daily goal row:** "Daily goal" and "E / G XP" (e.g. "15 / 20 XP") with a small bar;
   when this lesson takes the learner from below the goal to at or above it: "Daily goal
   reached!" and the value. If the goal was already reached earlier today, the row shows
   the value with a check icon and no celebration.
6. **Celebration:** when the streak was started/extended or the goal was newly reached,
   a banner appears above the stats with the matching line ("Streak extended!",
   "Daily goal reached!", or both as "Streak extended and daily goal reached!") and a
   sparkle effect of at most 1,000 ms. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` the banner
   shows without the effect.
7. The celebration text is announced once through a polite live region after the heading
   receives focus (LES-008 criterion 8 stays: focus on the heading).
8. No celebration is shown when neither event happened (e.g. a replay after the goal was
   reached and the streak already extended today).
9. The rows are part of the stats list, so a screen reader reads "Streak, 4 days" and
   "Daily goal, 15 of 20 XP".
10. If the earlier results can't be read, the Streak row shows "–" (accessible value
    "unavailable"), the Daily goal row shows this lesson's XP against the goal, no
    celebration is shown, and no extra error message appears.
11. If saving the result fails (LES-008 criterion 7), the rows and celebration still show
    as computed; the existing save-error alert explains the problem.
12. At 375 × 667 the whole results screen (Lumi, heading, banner, stats, two rows,
    "Continue") is still visible without scrolling. Lumi may drop from `lg` to `md` to
    make room.
13. Contrast 4.5:1 for all row text and 3:1 for the goal bar in both themes; no colour
    literals.
14. RTL tests cover: first lesson ever; first lesson today after yesterday; second lesson
    today; goal newly reached; goal already reached; both events; reduced motion; earlier
    results unreadable; save error with rows still visible.
15. The LES-008 Playwright test is extended: a new guest finishes Lesson 1 and sees
    "Streak started!", "1 day" and "15 / 20 XP" (default goal 20).

## Out of scope
- Streak freeze offers on the results screen (Phase 2)
- Sharing results (Phase 2)
- Sound for the celebration (LES-012, v1.0.0)
- "Save your progress" prompt (PLT-013, v0.4.0)

## UX notes
- **Success:** Lumi celebrating, "Lesson complete!", optional celebration banner (amber
  glow), XP / Accuracy / Time cards, then Streak and Daily goal rows, "Continue" pinned
  at the bottom.
- **Celebration:** short sparkle burst around the banner; no confetti over the content,
  nothing blocks the "Continue" button.
- **Loading:** not applicable (rows render with the stats).
- **Error:** criterion 10–11.
- **Empty:** not applicable.
