# PLT-011: Google sign-in
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** PLT-010, CTO-025 · **Milestone:**
v0.6.0 Accounts and sync. **Built and tested in part A** with a mocked provider; the live
Google check is **part B** (CTO-024, needs owner item O3) · **Decisions:** PD-071 #2, PD-087

## User story
As a learner, I want to sign in with Google so that I don't need another password (US-PLT-07).

## Acceptance criteria
1. When the Google flag (ADR-020) is on, `/sign-up` and `/log-in` show a "Continue with Google"
   button above the email form, separated by a divider "or". It follows Google's published
   button branding (the "G" mark, that exact label). When the flag is off, neither the button
   nor the divider is rendered.
2. Activating it starts Supabase OAuth with Google, redirecting back to `/auth/callback`. The
   button shows "Opening Google…" and is disabled until the page leaves.
3. `/auth/callback` exchanges the code, calls `finishSignIn()` (same path as email, so PLT-013's
   migration runs), then goes to `/`, or to `next` only when it is a same-origin path; any
   other `next` value is ignored (open-redirect test).
4. Cancelled at Google → back on `/log-in` with "Google sign-in was cancelled. You can try again
   or use your email." Any other provider error → "Google sign-in didn't work. Try again or use
   your email." No raw error text, no tokens in the URL after the callback finishes.
5. A Google email that already has a password account behaves as ADR-020 decides; the learner
   never silently ends up with two accounts. If Supabase refuses the link, the copy is "There's
   already an account with this email. Log in with your email and password."
6. Offline: the button is disabled under the PLT-010 offline message.
7. Tests: RTL with a mocked client for criteria 1–6 (redirect options, callback success, cancel,
   error, open-redirect guard). No live Google call in CI.
8. At 375 × 667 the button is full width and at least 48 px high; keyboard reachable before the
   email field; no serious or critical axe violations. `npm run typecheck`, `lint`, `test`,
   `build` pass.

## Out of scope
- Apple sign-in (Phase 2)
- The Google Cloud OAuth client and the Supabase provider settings (owner item O3, then CTO-024)

## UX notes

- **Error:** criterion 4, shown above the form on `/log-in`. **Success:** as email sign-in.
