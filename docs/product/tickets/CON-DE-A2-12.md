# CON-DE-A2-12: A2 Unit 12 "Offices and Letters": 5 original lessons (en → de)
**Owner:** pm (content writer) · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** CON-DE-A1-QA; appendices
of CON-DE-A2-01 … 11 (PD-052) · **Milestone:** v0.5.0 · **Wave:** 3 · **Curriculum:**
`curriculum-german.md` §4b Unit 12 · **Decisions:** PD-035, PD-045 … PD-052

## User story
As a newcomer, I want to understand short formal letters and emails, reply politely and know
which documents to bring, so that I can deal with offices, schools and my landlord.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** formal emails and letters, documents, opening hours, school and daycare, a
  notice from the property management
- **Life in DACH:** "Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren", bringing copies of documents, the
  daycare centre's parents' evening
- **Can-do:** I can understand the main point of a short formal letter or email, reply with
  the right greeting and closing, ask which documents I need, and say what happens if
  something is the case.
- **Grammar:** **`wenn`** (clause first: verb–comma–verb); **`deshalb`, `trotzdem`** (intro,
  verb in position 2); **`ob`** (intro); `wissen`.

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u12-l1-letters-and-emails` **"Letters and Emails"**: `Sehr geehrte Damen und
   Herren,`, `Sehr geehrte Frau Okafor,`, `Sehr geehrter Herr Wagner,`, `Mit freundlichen
   Grüßen`, `Vielen Dank für Ihre E-Mail.` is **not** allowed (`vielen Dank` not taught);
   use `Danke für Ihre E-Mail.`, `der Betreff`, `Ich antworte Ihnen morgen.`
2. `de-a2-u12-l2-documents` **"Documents"**: `Ich möchte mich anmelden.` (resolves A1
   native-review item 6), `Bringen Sie bitte Ihren Pass mit.`, `eine Kopie`, `Bitte
   unterschreiben Sie hier.`, `die Bescheinigung`, `Ich weiß es nicht.`
3. `de-a2-u12-l3-if-you-have-questions` **"If You Have Questions"**: `Wenn Sie Fragen haben,
   rufen Sie uns an.`, `Wenn ein Dokument fehlt, schicken wir Ihnen einen Brief.`, `Sie können
   das schriftlich oder persönlich machen.`, `die Öffnungszeiten`, `Das Amt ist am Freitag
   geschlossen` is **not** allowed (`das Amt` not taught); use `Das Bürgeramt ist am Freitag
   geschlossen.`
4. `de-a2-u12-l4-school-and-daycare` **"School and Daycare"**: `Mein Sohn ist krank, deshalb
   kommt er heute nicht in die Kita.`, `Es regnet, trotzdem gehen die Kinder raus` is **not**
   allowed (`raus` not taught); use `…, trotzdem spielen die Kinder draußen.`, `Ich weiß nicht,
   ob ich zum Elternabend kommen kann.`, `die Schule`.
5. `de-a2-u12-l5-letter-from-the-landlord` **"Scenario: A Letter from the Property
   Management"**: a notice says a tradesperson comes on Monday; the learner replies by
   email: `die Hausverwaltung`, `der Handwerker / die Handwerkerin`, `die Nebenkosten`,
   `informieren`, `Bitte beachten Sie: …`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u12`, title "Offices and Letters", description "Read and answer formal emails
   and letters, bring the right documents, and use wenn, deshalb and trotzdem.", in level A2
   after `de-a2-u11`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u12-l1-letters-and-emails` "Letters and Emails"
   2. `de-a2-u12-l2-documents` "Documents"
   3. `de-a2-u12-l3-if-you-have-questions` "If You Have Questions"
   4. `de-a2-u12-l4-school-and-daycare` "School and Daycare"
   5. `de-a2-u12-l5-letter-from-the-landlord` "Scenario: A Letter from the Property
      Management"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. `wenn` appears in at least 5 exercises, at least 2 with the clause first and the main
   verb straight after the comma (`Wenn …, rufen Sie …`); 1 multiple choice has the
   distractor `Wenn Sie Fragen haben, Sie rufen uns an.`.
4. `deshalb` and `trotzdem` appear at least 2 times each with the verb in position 2; 1
   multiple choice has the non-inverted distractor (`deshalb er kommt`).
5. `ob` appears in at least 2 exercises (intro only, after `Ich weiß nicht` or `Können Sie
   mir sagen`).
6. Formal greetings and the closing appear in at least 3 exercises, with `geehrte` for a
   woman and `geehrter` for a man; every word is its own tile.
7. Offices, schools and the property management use `Sie`. No legal claims: no deadlines,
   fines, required documents "by law", tenancy or utility-cost rules (PD-035, PD-051).
8. `sich anmelden` appears in at least 1 exercise.

Unit total: 32 items. `Damen` and `Herren` count only inside the greeting chunk.
`das Formular`, `ausfüllen`, `die Unterschrift`, `das Bürgeramt` (A1 U12), `der Brief` (U5),
`die Frage` (U11) and `schreiben` (U3) are recycled.

## Out of scope
- Writing a full formal letter or complaint (B1); typed answers (Phase 2)
- Residence permits, benefits, tax, insurance letters (B1+, no legal claims)
- `als` vs `wenn` for the past (B1)

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "wenn sends the verb to the end. Clause first? Then verb, comma, verb: Wenn
  Sie Fragen haben, rufen Sie an."; "After deshalb and trotzdem the verb comes next:
  Deshalb komme ich später."
- Letter prompts can show the greeting on its own line; answers are one sentence each.
