# LES-006: Match-pairs exercise
**Owner:** dev-lessons · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** CTO-002 (and the props interface in LES-002 Technical notes) · **Milestone:** v0.2.0

## User story
As a learner, I want to match Spanish words with their English translations so that I can
practise vocabulary quickly (US-LES-05).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Shows the instruction "Match the pairs" and the pairs from the content (5 in MVP
   content; the component supports 3 to 6) as tiles in two columns: Spanish on the left,
   English on the right. Each column is shuffled separately.
2. Selecting a tile highlights it (`aria-pressed="true"`). Selecting another tile in the
   same column moves the selection.
3. Selecting one tile in each column that belong together marks both as matched: disabled,
   success style and a check icon.
4. Selecting a non-matching pair shows both in the error style for about 600 ms, then
   clears the selection. They stay available.
5. When every pair is matched, the component reports "complete" together with whether any
   mismatch happened (PD-012). No "Check" button is needed.
6. Keyboard only: tiles are buttons in tab order; Enter or Space selects. Matched tiles
   are skipped in the tab order.
7. Screen readers hear each result: "Matched: hola, hello" or "Not a match".
8. Spanish tiles carry `lang="es"`.
9. State is shown by icon/style as well as colour; text contrast is at least 4.5:1 in all
   states.
10. With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, the mismatch shows without a shake animation.
11. At 375 px: both columns fit side by side, tiles are at least 44 px tall, and words up
    to 16 characters fit without being cut off (they may wrap).
12. Unit tests cover: shuffle keeps all pairs, match, mismatch, same-column reselect,
    completion with and without mismatches, keyboard flow.

## Out of scope
- Audio on tiles (Phase 2)
- Timed "speed round" matching (Phase 2)
- Hearts lost on mismatch (decided in v0.3.0: at most 1 heart per match-pairs exercise,
  PD-012)

## UX notes
- **Normal:** white tiles, indigo text. **Selected:** indigo border, light amber fill.
  **Matched:** success style, check icon, faded. **Mismatch:** error style briefly.
- **Success:** after the last pair, the player shows the correct feedback panel and
  "Continue".
- **Loading/empty/error:** handled by the player. With fewer than 3 pairs in the content,
  the component reports "skipped".
