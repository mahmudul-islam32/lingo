# PLT-017: Settings screen: daily goal, appearance (dark/light), account and log out
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** PLT-016, PLT-010, PLT-014, GAM-006, CTO-004 · **Milestone:** v0.4.0

## User story
As a learner, I want to change my daily goal and the app's appearance, and log out on a
shared device, so that the app fits my routine and my account stays safe (US-PLT-09,
US-PLT-12, US-GAM-06; PD-015).

## Context
PD-026 sets the v0.4.0 settings scope: daily goal, appearance, account and log out. The
sound-effects and speaking-exercise toggles arrive with the features they control
(LES-012, LES-011 in v1.0.0), so no switch does nothing today. Appearance is a device
preference, not synced (PD-027). The log-out rules are PD-028.

## Acceptance criteria
**Screen**
1. `/settings` opens from the Profile gear button (PLT-016 criterion 11, enabled by this
   ticket). It has an `<h1>` "Settings" and a back button (`aria-label="Back to
   profile"`) to `/profile`. The bottom navigation stays visible with Profile marked
   current. The route works offline.
2. If settings don't exist yet (onboarding not finished), `/settings` redirects to
   onboarding, the same rule as the other tabs (onboarding gate).

**Daily goal**
3. A "Daily goal" radio group lists 10, 20, 30 and 50 XP, with the same copy as
   onboarding (PLT-004 `GOAL_COPY`, e.g. "Casual", "10 XP", "About 1 lesson a day"). The current goal is checked, and
   arrow keys move between options (native radio behaviour).
4. Choosing a new option saves at once through `updateSettings` (which also enqueues
   the sync record) and shows the toast "Daily goal updated." There is no save button.
   While the save runs the group is `aria-busy`. If it fails, the selection goes back to
   the previous goal and the toast says "Couldn't save your goal. Try again."
5. The new goal applies to **today** right away (PD-029). The goal ring (GAM-006) and the
   results screen use it without a reload, so "reached" can become true or false. Today's
   XP and the streak don't change (tested: 20 XP earned today, goal 30 → 10 shows reached,
   goal 10 → 30 shows 20 / 30).

**Appearance (PD-015, PD-027)**
6. An "Appearance" radio group offers "Dark" and "Light". Dark is checked by default.
7. Choosing an option switches the whole app at once (`data-theme` on `<html>`, through
   the existing `useTheme()`), and stores it on the device (`localStorage`).
8. A reload or a new launch paints the stored theme on the **first** frame, with no flash
   of the other theme (tested: `data-theme` is already correct when `DOMContentLoaded`
   fires). This works offline and for guests.
9. The appearance is not synced, and it is kept on log out (device preference, PD-027).

**Account section**
10. **Signed in:** an "Account" section shows "Logged in as <email>" and a "Log out"
    button.
11. **Guest, accounts enabled:** the section shows "You're using Polyglow as a guest.
    Create an account to save your progress." with the "Create account" (→ `/signup`) and
    "Log in" (→ `/login`) buttons.
12. **`off` mode:** the Account section is not rendered at all.

**Log out (PD-028)**
13. "Log out" first runs one `syncNow()` attempt ("Logging out…" on the button, at most
    10 s, then it carries on).
14. If pending lesson results remain after that attempt (offline, or the sync failed), a
    dialog says "1 lesson hasn't synced yet" (or "N lessons…"), with "If you log out now,
    it will be lost. Connect to the internet to save it first." and the buttons "Stay
    logged in" (primary, default focus) and "Log out anyway" (destructive style). With
    nothing pending, there is no dialog.
15. Logging out signs out through the gateway, deletes all of the learner's local data
    (Dexie identity, profile, settings, hearts, results, sync queue and meta), clears the
    query cache, and creates a fresh guest. The learner lands on the onboarding welcome
    screen. The theme and the install-prompt dismissal stay (they're device preferences).
16. After log out, Learn and Profile show none of the account's data, even through the
    back button (tested: the old XP total is nowhere in the DOM). Logging in again (PLT-010
    + PLT-013) brings back the account's synced progress via the pull.
17. Log out works offline too: the dialog shows, and "Log out anyway" completes locally.

**Quality**
18. Keyboard only: every control can be reached in a logical order, both radio groups
    have visible group labels (`fieldset` / `legend`), and the dialog traps focus and
    closes on Escape (= "Stay logged in"). There are no serious or critical axe
    violations on the screen (guest, signed in, `off` mode) and in the dialog, in both
    themes. No horizontal scrolling at 375 × 667.
19. Playwright (mobile, demo mode):
    - **Goal:** change the goal to 10 on a day with 15 XP; Learn shows the goal reached.
    - **Theme:** switch to Light, reload, and `<html data-theme="light">` is there with no
      dark first paint.
    - **Log out:** a signed-in learner goes offline, finishes a lesson, presses "Log out",
      sees "1 lesson hasn't synced yet", and chooses "Stay logged in". Back online, "Log
      out" runs with no dialog and lands on onboarding. Logging back in shows the synced
      XP.

## Out of scope
- Sound effects and speaking exercises toggles (with LES-012 / LES-011, v1.0.0, PD-026)
- Changing the name or avatar (PLT-024, v1.0.0), email or password
- Following the OS colour scheme automatically (PD-015)
- Changing the course (one course only)
- Account deletion, data export

## UX notes
- **Layout (top to bottom):** header (back button, "Settings"), "Daily goal" card with the
  radio rows, "Appearance" card with two radio rows (each with a small swatch preview),
  "Account" card, then a muted footer with the app version (e.g. "Polyglow 0.4.0").
- **Loading:** skeleton rows inside each card, for the first read only.
- **Error:** if settings can't be read: "We couldn't load your settings." with "Try
  again".
- **Success:** toasts for saves. The theme changes instantly, with no toast.
- **Log out dialog:** uses the shared `Modal`. The destructive button uses the error
  token colour, plus its text label (not colour alone).
