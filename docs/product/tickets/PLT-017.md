# PLT-017: Log out and the Account settings rows
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** PLT-010, PLT-014, PLT-015 ·
**Milestone:** v0.6.0 Accounts and sync, part A (local Supabase) · **Decisions:** PD-066,
PD-087 (re-scoped: daily goal, sound and start level rows already exist; the appearance toggle
is dropped because the app is light-only)

## User story
As a signed-in learner, I want to log out so that I can protect my account on a shared device,
without losing anything that hasn't synced yet (US-PLT-09, US-AC-04).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Signed in, Profile's settings card gets an "Account" group with an **Email** row (read-only)
   and a **Log out** row. A guest or a build with accounts disabled shows neither.
2. "Log out" first runs `syncNow()` (at most 10 s, "Saving your progress…" on the row).
3. If nothing is pending afterwards, a dialog asks "Log out on this device?" with "Your progress
   is saved to your account. This device will start fresh." and "Log out" / "Cancel".
4. If records are still pending (offline or failing), the dialog says "<n> results haven't been
   saved to your account yet. If you log out now, they'll be lost." with **"Stay logged in"**
   (primary, default focus) and "Log out anyway" (destructive style).
5. Logging out signs out of Supabase on this device, clears the whole Dexie database, creates a
   fresh guest and opens onboarding's welcome step. Profile then shows "Guest" and 0 XP; the
   browser's Back button doesn't show the previous account's data.
6. Offline, logging out is still allowed (local sign-out) with the criterion 4 warning when
   records are pending.
7. Log back in on the same device → the account's progress returns through the pull (PLT-015).
8. No "Appearance" row is added (PD-066); the existing Daily goal, Start level, Goal date and
   Sound effects rows are unchanged.
9. e2e (`accounts` project): sign in, finish a lesson, log out → onboarding; log in → the lesson
   is completed again. Second e2e: offline, finish a lesson, Log out → the warning shows `1
   result`, "Stay logged in" keeps everything.
10. Dialogs: `role="alertdialog"` for criterion 4, focus trapped, Esc = Cancel / Stay logged in,
    48 px buttons, fit at 375 × 667. No serious or critical axe violations.
    `npm run typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, `build` pass.

## Out of scope
- Account deletion (PLT-032, planned for the v1.0.0 launch gate), changing email or password
- Logging out on all devices

## UX notes
- **Loading:** criterion 2. **Error:** criterion 4 is the only error state. **Success:**
  onboarding welcome. **Empty:** n/a.
