# LES-012: Sound effects
**Owner:** dev-lessons (Profile row: dev-platform) · **Priority:** P2 · **Depends on:** LES-002,
PLT-026 · **Milestone:** pulled forward from v1.0.0 at the owner's request ·
**Decisions:** PD-074 (replaces PD-067 §6)

## User story
As a learner, I want a short sound when I answer right or wrong and when I finish a lesson,
so that lessons feel like a game and I get feedback without reading the panel.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A correct Check plays a short, bright rising chime; a wrong Check plays a soft, low
   falling tone (never a harsh buzzer); finishing the lesson plays a short fanfare.
2. Explain cards, open tasks ("Show model answer") and skips play nothing. Match pairs chime
   when every pair is matched (the panel is always "correct" there).
3. The sounds are our own, synthesised with the Web Audio API: no audio files, no other
   app's sounds (CLAUDE.md brand rules), nothing to cache for offline.
4. The lesson reads `Settings.soundEffectsEnabled` (default on). Off → no sound at all.
5. Profile → Settings has a "Sound effects" row: On / Off and a "Turn off" / "Turn on"
   button (accessible names "Turn sound effects off" / "…on"). Turning on plays the correct
   chime once. A failed save shows an error and keeps the old value.
6. No Web Audio, a blocked or failing audio context never throws and never blocks the lesson.
7. Unit tests cover the sound mapping, the player wiring (on, off, complete once) and the
   Profile row (off, on with preview, save error).

## Out of scope
- A sound for each wrong pair inside match pairs, streak or goal sounds
- A volume slider; haptics / vibration
