# FIX-007: Offline banner axe check waits for the banner to settle
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P2 · **Depends on:** — · **Milestone:** v0.5.2
(released) · **Merged:** #78 · **Found in:** e2e flake in CI (recorded after the fact, PM-ACC4)

## User story
As a developer, I want the offline banner's accessibility check to read the banner's final
colours, so that the axe check doesn't fail during the banner's entry transition.

## What was fixed
`tests/e2e/offline-banner.spec.ts` waits until the banner's transition has finished before
running axe. The banner itself did not change.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The axe check on the offline banner runs only after the banner has settled.
2. The spec passes repeatedly on the production build with no serious or critical axe
   violations.
3. No change to the banner's copy, look or behaviour.

## Out of scope
Restyling the banner (PLT-026).
