## User story
As a learner, I want to make, move and cancel appointments on the phone and explain why I
couldn't come, so that I can handle practices, offices and services without help.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** phone calls, voicemail, minutes and hours, moving and cancelling appointments,
  excuses and reasons
- **Life in DACH:** calling a practice or a hairdresser, the formal phone greeting with your
  surname, leaving a message
- **Can-do:** I can make, move or cancel an appointment on the phone, leave a short message,
  apologise and give a reason, and say what I could, had to or wanted to do.
- **Grammar:** **Präteritum of modal verbs** `konnte`, `musste`, `wollte`, `durfte`;
  **`weil`** with the verb at the end; minutes and hours (`die Minute(n)`, PD-042).

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u08-l1-on-the-phone` **"On the Phone"**: `Praxis Dr. …` is **not** allowed (no
   titles or abbreviations); use `Hier spricht Yusuf Demir.`, `Moment, bitte.`, `Kann ich
   eine Nachricht hinterlassen?` is **not** allowed (`hinterlassen` not taught); use `Bitte
   rufen Sie mich zurück.`, `Die Nummer ist besetzt.`, `Auf Wiederhören!`.
2. `de-a2-u08-l2-minutes-and-hours` **"Minutes and Hours"**: `in zehn Minuten`, `in einer
   Stunde`, `eine halbe Stunde`, `Ich komme gleich.`, `Ich komme später.`, `Ich bin zu
   spät.` Times and numbers in words.
3. `de-a2-u08-l3-moving-an-appointment` **"Moving an Appointment"**: `Ich möchte meinen
   Termin verschieben.`, `Ich muss den Termin leider absagen.`, `Passt Ihnen Dienstag am
   Vormittag?`, `Ist das möglich?`, `der Friseur / die Friseurin`.
4. `de-a2-u08-l4-because` **"Because I Was Ill"**: `Warum bist du nicht gekommen? – Weil ich
   krank war.`, `Ich konnte nicht kommen, weil ich arbeiten musste.`, `Ich wollte anrufen,
   aber ich habe es vergessen.`, `Ich durfte nicht joggen.`
5. `de-a2-u08-l5-voicemail` **"Scenario: A Message on the Mailbox"**: a learner calls a
   friend and a practice, reaches the voicemail, leaves a message and says they'll get in
   touch: `die Mailbox`, `Bescheid sagen`, `erreichen`, `sich melden`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u08`, title "Appointments and Phone Calls", description "Make, move and cancel
   appointments by phone, leave a message and give reasons with weil.", in level A2 after
   `de-a2-u07`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u08-l1-on-the-phone` "On the Phone"
   2. `de-a2-u08-l2-minutes-and-hours` "Minutes and Hours"
   3. `de-a2-u08-l3-moving-an-appointment` "Moving an Appointment"
   4. `de-a2-u08-l4-because` "Because I Was Ill"
   5. `de-a2-u08-l5-voicemail` "Scenario: A Message on the Mailbox"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. `weil` appears in at least 6 word banks with the verb at the end, including at least 1
   separable verb kept together at the end (`weil ich früh aufstehe`) and at least 2 with
   `war` / a Perfekt in the clause; at least 1 multiple choice has the V2 distractor (`weil
   ich war krank`). Clause-first answers are accepted where the tiles allow (CDS-19.4).
4. `konnte`, `musste`, `wollte`, `durfte` each appear at least once, at least 6 exercises in
   total, with the infinitive at the end.
5. Minutes and hours appear in at least 5 exercises, in words; `in` + time in at least 2.
6. At least 3 listen exercises are original phone or voicemail lines (one or two sentences).
7. Calls to practices, salons and offices use `Sie`; calls to friends use `du`; prompts mark
   the register.
8. No real practice names, no doctor titles or abbreviations (`Dr.`), no cancellation-fee
   claims (PD-051).

- `dass` (U11), `wenn` (U12)
- `sollte` (B1, Konjunktiv II meaning), `möchte` in the past
- Train announcements with minutes (U10)
