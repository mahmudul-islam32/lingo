## User story
As a traveller in German-speaking countries, I want to understand platform and delay
announcements, deal with a missed connection and book and check into a hotel, so that I
travel on my own with confidence.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** long-distance train travel, seat reservations, announcements, delays and
  missed connections, booking and checking into a hotel
- **Life in DACH:** a platform change announced at the last minute, a reserved seat in
  carriage 7, a late check-in
- **Can-do:** I can understand platform and delay announcements with minutes, find my
  carriage and seat, book a hotel room, check in and ask about the room.
- **Grammar:** **`auf Gleis` / `von Gleis` + number** and **`der Bahnsteig`** (completes
  PD-042); **`… Minuten Verspätung`**; two-way and dative prepositions applied; hundreds in
  prices; Perfekt and `konnte` in problem reports; adjective endings recycled.

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u10-l1-which-platform` **"Which Platform?"**: `Der Zug nach Basel fährt von Gleis
   acht.`, `Der Zug steht auf Gleis drei.`, `Wo ist der Bahnsteig?`, `Ich habe einen Platz
   reserviert.`, `Wagen sieben, Platz zweiundvierzig.`
2. `de-a2-u10-l2-ten-minutes-late` **"Ten Minutes Late"**: `Der Zug hat zehn Minuten
   Verspätung.`, `Der Zug fällt heute aus.`, `Ich habe meinen Anschluss verpasst.`, `die
   Abfahrt`, `die Ankunft`, announcements (`Achtung` is **not** taught; start with the
   train: `Der Zug nach Linz fährt heute von Gleis vier.`).
3. `de-a2-u10-l3-booking-a-room` **"Booking a Room"**: `Ich möchte ein Einzelzimmer für zwei
   Nächte buchen.`, `Das Doppelzimmer kostet hundertzwanzig Euro pro Nacht.`, `mit
   Frühstück`, `Haben Sie ein ruhiges Zimmer?` (U6 endings), `die Bestätigung`.
4. `de-a2-u10-l4-reception` **"At Reception"**: `Ihr Zimmer ist im dritten Stock.`, `Der
   Aufzug ist links.`, `Die Dusche funktioniert nicht.`, `Wie ist das Passwort für das
   WLAN?`, `Ich brauche ein Handtuch.`
5. `de-a2-u10-l5-missed-connection` **"Scenario: Missed Connection"**: the train is late, the
   learner misses the connection and calls the hotel: `Ich komme heute Nacht später an.`,
   `Ich konnte den Zug nicht nehmen.`, `Kann ich mit Kreditkarte bezahlen?`, `das Problem`,
   `stornieren`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u10`, title "Train and Hotel", description "Understand platforms and delays,
   deal with a missed connection, and book and check into a hotel.", in level A2 after
   `de-a2-u09`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u10-l1-which-platform` "Which Platform?"
   2. `de-a2-u10-l2-ten-minutes-late` "Ten Minutes Late"
   3. `de-a2-u10-l3-booking-a-room` "Booking a Room"
   4. `de-a2-u10-l4-reception` "At Reception"
   5. `de-a2-u10-l5-missed-connection` "Scenario: Missed Connection"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. `auf Gleis` and `von Gleis` + number appear in at least 5 exercises, including at least 3
   listen exercises that are original announcements of one or two sentences; `der
   Bahnsteig` in at least 2. Platform, carriage and seat numbers are words.
4. `… Minuten Verspätung` appears in at least 3 exercises (completes PD-042).
5. Hotel prices with hundreds appear in at least 2 exercises (number tiles ≤ 24 characters,
   CDS-18).
6. Two-way and dative prepositions are recycled in at least 4 exercises (`im Hotel`, `ins
   Hotel`, `vom Bahnhof zum Hotel`, `im dritten Stock`); adjective endings in at least 2.
7. No real train operators, train categories, line numbers, timetables, hotel chains,
   passenger-rights or refund rules (CDS-8, PD-051).
8. Reception and phone calls to the hotel use `Sie`.

## Out of scope
- `Achtung`, `Bitte Vorsicht` and other station phrases beyond the appendix
- Refunds, compensation, passenger rights (no legal claims)
- Reviews, complaints in writing (B1)

## UX notes

  Der Zug hat zehn Minuten Verspätung."; "Floors use ordinals: im zweiten Stock."

### Uncertainties / needs native review
1. **`Ich habe eine Reservierung für Wagen sieben.`** (L1 ex08): natural, but native speakers may
   prefer `in Wagen sieben` or `einen Platz in Wagen sieben reserviert`.
2. **`in` + `Wagen` + number** is not used anywhere (`in Wagen sieben` vs `im Wagen sieben` varies);
   carriage numbers appear only bare (`Wagen sieben, Platz zweiundvierzig`).
3. L1 ex03 and L1 ex10 avoid `an Gleis drei` / `am Bahnsteig` as distractors, because both are
   correct German. L1 ex10 offers `auf Bahnsteig` (no article) as a learner error modelled on
   `auf Gleis`.
4. L4 ex07 / ex12 translate `im zweiten / vierten Stock` as "second / fourth floor" (British
   counting). US learners count one higher; the tip doesn't explain this.
5. L5 ex12 accepts five orders, including the fronted `Zum Hotel nehme ich die Straßenbahn vom
   Bahnhof.` (PD-043 rule 2) which is grammatical but marked. `Ich nehme die Straßenbahn zum Hotel
   vom Bahnhof.` is buildable and rejected.
6. L3 ex11 accepts the object-first `Ein ruhiges Doppelzimmer haben wir gebucht.` (indefinite
   object, contrastive). L3 ex04 rejects the price-first `Hundertzwanzig Euro kostet …`.
7. L5 ex06 accepts `Ich komme später heute Nacht an.`; native review should confirm it.
8. L1 ex12 accepts only `The platform is on the left.`; `The platform is left.` is buildable and
   rejected as unnatural.
9. **Unit 9 assumption:** U9 is still being written, so I used `funktionieren`, `neben` (+ dative)
   and `der Schlüssel` from its planned CON-DE-A2-09 appendix (L4 ex04, ex06, ex09, ex10). If U9
   changes them, those exercises need a recheck.
10. `Achtung` and other station phrases are not used (out of scope); announcements start with the
    train (`Der Zug nach …`).
