# PLT-015: Sync status in the offline indicator, reconnect flow and Unit 1 audio warm-up
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** PLT-014, PLT-006, PLT-007, PLT-002 · **Milestone:** v0.4.0

## User story
As a signed-in learner, I want to see when progress is waiting to sync and when it has
been saved, and I want Unit 1's audio to keep working offline, so that I trust that a
lesson finished on the train is never lost (US-PLT-14, US-PLT-15, US-PLT-16; PRD G3).

## Context
The ticket was reserved as "Offline caching of Unit 1 lessons and audio, sync on
reconnect". Unit 1 lesson pages and JSON chunks are already precached since PLT-007
(accepted criterion 7), so this ticket covers what is left (PD-023):
- the sync status in the existing banner (PLT-006),
- the reconnect flow end to end,
- the audio runtime cache and warm-up (ARCHITECTURE §10).

There are no licensed audio files yet (PRD Q7). The warm-up must tolerate missing files,
and its tests use fixture audio.

## Acceptance criteria
**Sync status (accounts only)**
1. For a guest, or in `off` mode, the banner behaves exactly as in PLT-006. Its texts and
   tests are unchanged.
2. Signed in and offline with N ≥ 1 pending lesson results, the banner says "You're
   offline. 1 lesson will sync when you're back online." (or "N lessons…"). With N = 0 it
   keeps the PLT-006 text.
3. On reconnect with pending records: "Back online" (PLT-006), then "Syncing your
   progress…" while `status()` is `syncing`, then "All progress saved", which disappears
   after 3 s. With nothing pending, only "Back online" shows (PLT-006 timing).
4. If the run ends in `error` with records still pending, the banner says "Couldn't sync
   yet. We'll keep trying." with a "Retry" button that calls `syncNow()`. The banner
   hides after the next successful run.
5. If the session can't be refreshed (PLT-014 criterion 8), the banner says "Log in again
   to keep syncing your progress." with a "Log in" link (→ `/login`). Logging in to the
   **same** account resumes syncing, and no data is lost. Logging in as a different
   account is blocked by PLT-013 criterion 4.
6. The banner stays hidden on `/lesson/*` (PLT-006 criterion 8). Status changes are
   announced once each through the existing `role="status"` region, and the text never
   overlaps the bottom navigation.

**Audio**
7. The service worker caches `/audio/**` CacheFirst in a cache named
   `audio-v<course.version>` with at most 300 entries. On activate, it deletes audio caches
   for other course versions.
8. After the first lesson of the course loads online, on an idle callback, the app fetches
   every unique audio URL (`src` and `slowSrc`) referenced by the Unit 1 lessons into that
   cache, once per course version (a stored marker). A missing file (404) or a failed file
   is skipped silently and doesn't stop the others. Nothing is fetched when
   `navigator.connection.saveData` is true.
9. Offline, a listen exercise whose audio was warmed plays from the cache. An uncached
   file falls back to speech synthesis exactly as in PD-007 (unchanged).

**End to end**
10. Playwright `pwa-prod` (demo mode, production build, mobile): a signed-in demo learner
    visits Learn online, goes offline (`context.setOffline(true)`), completes Lesson 1 with
    all answers correct, sees "1 lesson will sync…" on Learn, and goes back online. Then:
    "All progress saved" appears, the demo backend holds exactly 1 result for Lesson 1, and
    after a reload the total XP is 15 (not 30).
11. Playwright `pwa-prod` with a fixture audio file: after Lesson 1 loads online, the
    `audio-v<version>` cache contains the fixture URL, and the file plays offline.
12. There are no serious or critical axe violations on Learn in each banner state from
    criteria 2–5. No horizontal scrolling at 375 × 667.

## Out of scope
- Precaching lessons (done, PLT-007)
- Generating or licensing audio (cto + owner, PRD Q7)
- A sync history or "last synced" screen
- Showing rejected records to learners (they're kept for diagnostics only, ADR-003)
- Background Sync while the app is closed

## UX notes
- **States (banner):** offline + pending · syncing · saved (3 s) · couldn't sync + Retry ·
  log in again. One line at 375 px where possible, wrapping to two lines max. Cloud icons
  from PLT-006, plus a spinner for syncing (static under reduced motion).
- **Copy:** calm and specific ("1 lesson will sync"), never alarming ("not lost").
- **Retry:** an inline text button inside the banner, ≥ 44 × 44 px target.
