# PLT-015: Sync engine, pull: continue on a second device
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** PLT-014, PLT-013 · **Milestone:**
v0.6.0 Accounts and sync, part A (local Supabase) · **Decisions:** PD-087 (re-scoped: offline
caching of all German lessons moves to PLT-031)

## User story
As a learner, I want to sign in on my second phone or laptop and carry on exactly where I left
off, so that my progress isn't tied to one device (US-AC-01).

## Acceptance criteria
1. After every successful push, after migration (PLT-013) and right after log-in, the engine
   pulls: lesson results with `created_at` after the results cursor, and profile, settings and
   hearts with `server_updated_at` after their cursor (server timestamps, ADR-003). It pages
   until done, so more than 1,000 results arrive (test with 1,200 seeded results).
2. **Merge:** results are a union by id and a local result is never deleted; settings, profile
   and hearts are last-write-wins by `updatedAt`, and a newer local record that is still pending
   is kept and pushed. Every remote row is parsed with its zod schema; an invalid row is skipped
   and reported, and the rest of the pull continues.
3. The cursors advance only after the merge is stored; a failed or offline pull leaves them as
   they were and retries on the next trigger.
4. After a merge the `progress`, `settings`, `profile` and `hearts` queries are invalidated, so
   Learn, Practice and Profile update without a reload.
5. **Second device (e2e, two browser contexts, `accounts` project):** device A, signed in,
   finishes 3 lessons, sets start level A2 and a goal date for German. Device B (fresh) logs in
   → within 10 s Learn on B shows the same completed lessons, path frontier, total XP, streak,
   daily goal, start level, goal-date card and Review card count as A.
6. **Carry on:** a lesson finished on B appears as completed on A after A's next sync (focus,
   60 s tick or `online`) without a reload, and A's total XP goes up by that result's XP only.
7. **Streak across devices:** a day played only on B counts for the streak on A (derived from the
   union, PD-003); unit test with results from two devices on consecutive days.
8. The account's active course (`settings.courseId`) and per-course goal plans are applied on
   B; a course that B's build doesn't offer falls back as today (hidden courses still load).
9. PLT-013 criterion 11's screen check (results of an existing account and the guest shown
   together after log-in) is part of this ticket's e2e.
10. Unit tests for the merge rules (union, LWW both ways, pending local wins, invalid row skipped,
    cursor not advanced on failure). `npm run typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, `build` pass.

## Out of scope
- Realtime push from the server (Phase 2, leaderboards)
- Caching all 130 German lessons and their audio offline (moved to **PLT-031**, planned with
  the TTS pipeline in v1.0.0, PD-087)
- Deleting results on the server

## UX notes
- **Loading:** the first pull after log-in shows "Loading your progress…" (`role="status"`) with
  Lumi on Learn until it finishes or fails (max 10 s, then Learn shows local data and the sync
  line in Profile says "Sync paused. We'll try again soon.").
- **Error / success:** shown through PLT-016's sync line; no toasts for background pulls.
