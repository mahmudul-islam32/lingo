# CON-DE-A2-01: A2 Unit 1 "Weather and Seasons": 5 original lessons (en → de)
**Owner:** pm (content writer) · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** CON-DE-A1-QA (A1 taught
inventory) · **Milestone:** v0.5.0 · **Wave:** 1 · **Curriculum:** `curriculum-german.md`
§4b Unit 1 · **Decisions:** PD-039, PD-045 … PD-052

## User story
As a learner who has finished A1, I want to talk about the weather and the seasons and say
in which month something happens, so that I can make small talk and plan things around the
German weather.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** weather, seasons, months with `im`, birthdays, a weekend forecast
- **Life in DACH:** rainy autumns, snowy Alpine winters, long summer evenings; checking the
  forecast before a Sunday hike
- **Can-do:** I can say what the weather is like, say what it is usually like in each
  season, say in which month or season something happens, and link two ideas with `aber`,
  `oder` and `denn`.
- **Grammar:** weather verbs with `es`; `im` + month and season (`im` = `in dem`, PD-039);
  `aber`, `oder`, `denn` in position 0; `immer`, `manchmal`, `nie`; temperatures in words.

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u01-l1-weather` **"What's the Weather Like?"**: `Wie ist das Wetter?`, `Die Sonne
   scheint.`, `Es regnet.`, `Es ist warm / kalt / windig.` (`kalt` from A1). Seeds: a phone
   call between cities (`In Hamburg regnet es.`), today vs yesterday (`heute`, `gestern`).
2. `de-a2-u01-l2-seasons` **"Seasons"**: the four seasons with `der`, `im Sommer`, `im
   Winter`, `Es schneit.`, `der Schnee`. Seeds: `Im Winter fahre ich gern Ski` is **not**
   allowed (Ski not taught); use `Im Winter wandere ich nicht.`, `Im Sommer schwimme ich oft.`
3. `de-a2-u01-l3-birthdays` **"Birthdays and Months"**: `im` + month with all 12 months
   (A1), `Wann hast du Geburtstag? – Im Juli.`, `am` + ordinal (A1) vs `im` + month,
   `die Woche`, `der Tag`, `immer`, `manchmal`, `nie`, `schon`. The tip explains `im` =
   `in dem`.
4. `de-a2-u01-l4-but-or-because` **"But, Or, Because"**: `aber`, `oder`, `denn` (no change
   to word order), `sonnig`, `bewölkt`, `zwanzig Grad`, `minus fünf Grad`. Seeds: `Es ist
   sonnig, aber kalt.`, `Wir gehen in den Park` is **not** allowed (U9); use `Wir bleiben
   heute drinnen` only in L5 after `drinnen` is taught.
5. `de-a2-u01-l5-forecast` **"Scenario: The Weekend Forecast"**: two friends (du) plan a
   Sunday hike, read and hear a short forecast, decide to stay inside or go: `der
   Wetterbericht`, `das Gewitter`, `heute Abend`, `draußen`, `drinnen`, `vielleicht`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u01`, title "Weather and Seasons", description "Talk about the weather and
   the seasons, say when things happen, and link ideas with aber, oder and denn.", the
   **first unit of level A2** in `course.json` (after `de-a1-u12`), with these lessons in
   order:
   1. `de-a2-u01-l1-weather` "What's the Weather Like?"
   2. `de-a2-u01-l2-seasons` "Seasons"
   3. `de-a2-u01-l3-birthdays` "Birthdays and Months"
   4. `de-a2-u01-l4-but-or-because` "But, Or, Because"
   5. `de-a2-u01-l5-forecast` "Scenario: The Weekend Forecast"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass (`curriculum-german.md` §7).
3. **`im` + month or season** appears in at least 6 exercises across L2–L5, with at least 4
   different months or seasons. At least 1 multiple choice contrasts `im Mai` with `am Mai`
   and `in Mai`. The L3 tip explains `im` = `in dem`. `im` is used with **no other noun** in
   this unit (PD-039, CDS-19.5).
4. Weather with `es` appears in at least 5 exercises (`es regnet`, `es schneit`, `es ist
   …`), and 1 multiple choice has a distractor without `es` (`Heute regnet.`).
5. `aber`, `oder` and `denn` each appear in at least 2 word banks. At least 1 multiple choice
   has a distractor that puts the verb at the end after `denn` (`…, denn es kalt ist.`).
6. Temperatures are written in words (`zwanzig Grad`, `minus fünf Grad`); no digits in tiles
   or answers.
7. At least 3 listen exercises in L5 are short, original forecast lines (one or two
   sentences, e.g. `Am Sonntag ist es sonnig, aber am Abend gibt es vielleicht ein
   Gewitter.`).
8. No `werden` future (`Es wird regnen.` is U14). Forecasts use the present tense with a
   time word or `vielleicht` (CDS-19.3).

Unit total: 36 items. `kalt`, `heiß`, `heute`, `gestern`, `morgen`, the months and `am` +
ordinal are A1. `schon` and `schön` may now be contrasted as distractors (curriculum §5).

## Out of scope
- `werden` (`Es wird kalt.`, U14) and the Präteritum `war` (U2)
- Other `in` / `an` / `auf` + article phrases (U9), `in den Bergen` (U3 uses `am Meer` only)
- Climate and environment topics (B1)
- Snow sports vocabulary (`Ski fahren`): not taught at A2

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "Weather uses es: Es regnet. Es ist warm."; "im = in dem. Use it with
  months and seasons: im Mai, im Winter. Dates keep am: am dritten Mai."; "aber, oder and
  denn don't change the word order: Es ist kalt, aber die Sonne scheint."
- Forecast audio: calm, clear voice (PD-034); no jingles.
