# CON-DE-A2-11: A2 Unit 11 "Work and Job Search": 5 original lessons (en → de)
**Owner:** pm (content writer) · **Priority:** P0 · **Depends on:** CON-DE-A1-QA; appendices
of CON-DE-A2-01 … 10 (PD-052) · **Milestone:** v0.5.0 · **Wave:** 3 · **Curriculum:**
`curriculum-german.md` §4b Unit 11 · **Decisions:** PD-035, PD-045 … PD-052

## User story
As a skilled worker looking for a job, I want to understand a job ad, talk about my training
and experience and answer simple interview questions, so that I can apply for work in a
German-speaking country.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** job ads, applications, CV and certificates, the interview, the job offer
- **Life in DACH:** a job ad with full-time or part-time, a CV with photo optional, an
  interview with `Sie`
- **Can-do:** I can understand a simple job ad, say what training and experience I have,
  answer simple interview questions, and give an opinion with "I think that …".
- **Grammar:** **`dass`** after `glauben`, `denken`, `hoffen`, `finden` (verb at the end,
  incl. modal + infinitive and Perfekt); `weil` recycled; reflexive `sich bewerben`, `sich
  vorstellen`; Perfekt for experience (`Ich habe drei Jahre als … gearbeitet.`).

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u11-l1-job-ads` **"Job Ads"**: `Die Firma sucht eine Krankenpflegerin in Vollzeit.`,
   `Die Stelle ist in Teilzeit.`, `Sie haben zwei Jahre Erfahrung.`, `Was verdient man?` is
   **not** allowed (`man` only in the A1 chunk); use `Wie viel verdiene ich?`, `das Gehalt`.
2. `de-a2-u11-l2-applying` **"Applying"**: `Ich bewerbe mich als Ingenieur.`, `Ich habe mich
   bei einer Firma in Köln beworben.`, `Mein Lebenslauf ist im Anhang` is **not** allowed
   (`Anhang` not taught); use `Hier ist mein Lebenslauf.`, `das Zeugnis`, `die Ausbildung`,
   `Ich habe in Indien studiert.`
3. `de-a2-u11-l3-i-think-that` **"I Think That…"**: `Ich glaube, dass die Stelle interessant
   ist.`, `Ich finde, dass Erfahrung wichtig ist.`, `Ich hoffe, dass ich die Stelle
   bekomme` is **not** allowed (`bekommen` not taught); use `Ich hoffe, dass die Firma mich
   einlädt.`, `Ich denke, dass das richtig ist.`
4. `de-a2-u11-l4-interview` **"The Interview"**: `Stellen Sie sich bitte vor.`, `Welche
   Sprachen sprechen Sie?`, `Ich bin zuverlässig und flexibel.`, `Was sind Ihre Stärken?`,
   `Haben Sie noch Fragen?`, `Ich glaube, dass ich gut im Team arbeiten kann.` (U9 `im`).
5. `de-a2-u11-l5-job-offer` **"Scenario: The Job Offer"**: the learner gets a reply after the
   interview, talks to a friend about it, meets the boss: `die Zusage`, `die Absage`, `der
   Vertrag`, `der Chef / die Chefin`, `das Team`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u11`, title "Work and Job Search", description "Read job ads, talk about your
   experience, get through an interview and say what you think with dass.", in level A2
   after `de-a2-u10`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u11-l1-job-ads` "Job Ads"
   2. `de-a2-u11-l2-applying` "Applying"
   3. `de-a2-u11-l3-i-think-that` "I Think That…"
   4. `de-a2-u11-l4-interview` "The Interview"
   5. `de-a2-u11-l5-job-offer` "Scenario: The Job Offer"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. `dass` appears in at least 6 word banks with the verb at the end, including at least 2
   with modal + infinitive at the end (`dass ich gut Deutsch sprechen kann`) and at least 1
   with a Perfekt (`dass ich … gearbeitet habe`); 1 multiple choice has the V2 distractor.
   The `dass` clause always follows the main clause (CDS-19.4).
4. `weil` (U8) is recycled in at least 2 exercises.
5. `sich bewerben` appears in at least 3 exercises, including 1 Perfekt (`Ich habe mich …
   beworben.`).
6. Every job noun accepts both forms (CDS-11); `der Chef / die Chefin` both appear.
7. Salaries and hours are invented and written in words; no statements about minimum wage,
   contracts, visas or work permits (PD-035, PD-051).
8. Interview questions avoid age, religion, health, family plans and origin as topics of
   evaluation (CDS-11).

| 1 | Job Ads | die Stelle (the job / position), die Stellenanzeige (the job ad), Vollzeit / in Vollzeit (full-time), Teilzeit / in Teilzeit (part-time), die Erfahrung (experience), verdienen (to earn), das Gehalt (the salary) | 7 |
| 2 | Applying | sich bewerben: ich bewerbe mich / beworben (to apply), die Bewerbung (the application), der Lebenslauf (the CV / résumé), das Zeugnis (the certificate / reference), die Ausbildung (training / vocational training), das Studium (university studies), studieren (to study, at university) | 7 |
| 3 | I Think That… | dass (that, verb at the end), glauben (to believe / think), denken (to think), hoffen (to hope), finden (to think, opinion: Ich finde, dass …; new meaning, "find" is A1), wichtig (important), richtig (right / correct) | 7 |
| 4 | The Interview | das Vorstellungsgespräch (the job interview), sich vorstellen (to introduce oneself), die Frage (the question), die Sprache / die Sprachen (the language / languages), zuverlässig (reliable), flexibel (flexible), die Stärke (the strength) | 7 |
| 5 | The Job Offer | die Zusage (the acceptance / job offer), die Absage (the rejection), der Vertrag (the contract), der Chef / die Chefin (the boss), das Team (the team) | 5 |

Unit total: 33 items. A1 jobs (`Krankenpfleger/in`, `Ingenieur/in`, `Lehrer/in`), `als`,
`die Firma`, `der Kollege / die Kollegin` and `wollen` are recycled. `Vollzeit` and `Teilzeit`
are used without an article, as in real ads; in match pairs they appear as `in Vollzeit` /
`in Teilzeit` (CDS-6 exception, noted for QA).

## Out of scope
- Writing a full application letter (B1), salary negotiation, employment law
- `um … zu`, `damit` (B1)
- Unemployment and job-centre topics (B1)

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "After dass the verb goes to the end: Ich glaube, dass die Stelle gut ist.";
  "With a modal, the modal is last: …, dass ich gut Deutsch sprechen kann."
- Tone: encouraging; the scenario ends with a `Zusage`, and a `Absage` appears only in a
  neutral exercise.
