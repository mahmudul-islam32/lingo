# PLT-016: Profile account card: save-your-progress entry, signed-in email, sync status
**Owner:** dev-platform · **Priority:** P1 · **Depends on:** PLT-010, PLT-013, PLT-014 ·
**Milestone:** v0.6.0 Accounts and sync, part A (local Supabase) · **Decisions:** PD-066,
PD-087 (re-scoped: the Profile screen itself exists since PLT-026; this ticket adds the account
card)

## User story
As a learner, I want my profile to show whether my progress is saved to an account and synced,
so that I know it's safe and can fix it if it isn't (US-PLT-04, US-PLT-16, US-AC-02).

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Accounts disabled:** Profile is unchanged (no card; existing Profile tests pass unchanged).
2. **Guest:** a card under the stat tiles, title "Save your progress", text "Create an account to
   keep your XP, streak and goal date on every device.", a "Create account" button (`/sign-up`)
   and a "Log in" link (`/log-in`).
3. **Signed in:** the card shows "Signed in as <email>" and one sync line:
   - queue empty and last push succeeded → "All progress saved";
   - syncing → "Saving…";
   - offline with pending records → "You're offline. <n> results will sync when you're back
     online." (`<n>` = pending lesson results; "1 result" in the singular);
   - status `error` → "Sync paused. We'll try again soon." with "Try now" (calls `syncNow()`);
   - session `expired` → "Log in again to keep syncing." with a "Log in" button to `/log-in`
     (same account; nothing is lost).
4. The sync line is a polite live region and is announced only when its text changes (not on
   every 60 s tick).
5. Long emails truncate in the middle with an ellipsis and the full email is in the accessible
   name; nothing overflows at 375 px.
6. The header (avatar, display name, "Learning German · Joined …") is unchanged; for a migrated
   account "Joined" keeps the guest's original date (`joinedAt`, earliest wins).
7. RTL tests for every state in criteria 1–3 and the live-region rule; e2e (`accounts` project):
   signed in, Profile shows the email and "All progress saved"; offline after a lesson shows the
   pending count; back online returns to "All progress saved" within 10 s.
8. At 375 × 667 the card fits without horizontal scroll and buttons are at least 48 px; the
   state is never shown by colour alone; no serious or critical axe violations.
   `npm run typecheck`, `lint`, `test`, `build` pass.

## Out of scope
- Log out and the Account settings rows (PLT-017)
- Editing display name or avatar (US-PLT-11, P2), account deletion (PLT-032, planned)

## UX notes
- White Card like the settings rows, Lumi lantern icon on the guest card, a small check icon on
  "All progress saved", a cloud-off icon offline.
- **Loading:** a one-line skeleton until the session and queue are read. **Error / success /
  empty:** criterion 3.
