## User story
As a learner, I want to talk about sport and how I feel, give friends simple advice and
repeat what the doctor told me, so that I can look after myself and others.

## Unit outline
- **Theme:** sport and clubs, feeling well or ill, colds and small injuries, advice
- **Life in DACH:** the local sports club (`der Verein`) and its team; being signed off sick
- **Can-do:** I can say how I feel and what I do to stay fit, give friends simple advice,
  describe a cold or a small injury, and say what the doctor told me to do.
- **Grammar:** **reflexive verbs** with accusative reflexive pronouns (`mich, dich, sich,
  uns, euch, sich`); **imperative `du` and `ihr`**, stem changes (`Nimm!`, `Iss!`, `Lies!`,
  `Hilf!`), `Sei! / Seid!`; **`sollen`**.

## Lesson outline
1. `de-a2-u07-l1-sport` **"Sport"**: `Ich mache gern Sport.`, `Wir joggen morgens.`, `Ich
   trainiere am Dienstag und am Freitag.`, `Unsere Mannschaft spielt am Sonntag.`, `Sport ist
   gesund.` (`im Verein` is **not** allowed: `im` + noun is U9; `Ich bin in einem Verein.`
   isn't either; use `Mein Verein hat eine Mannschaft.`)
2. `de-a2-u07-l2-how-do-you-feel` **"How Do You Feel?"**: `Ich fühle mich nicht gut.`, `Wie
   fühlst du dich?`, `Er fühlt sich müde.`, `Es geht mir besser.`, `Ruh dich aus!` is **not**
   allowed yet (imperative is L3); use `Ich ruhe mich heute aus.`
3. `de-a2-u07-l3-drink-water` **"Drink Water!"**: `Trink viel Wasser!`, `Bleib zu Hause!`,
   `Macht eine Pause!`, `Nimm eine Tablette!`, `Iss etwas!` is **not** allowed (`etwas`
   only in the A1 chunk `sonst noch etwas`); use `Iss viel Obst!`, `Sei vorsichtig!`, `Ruh
   dich aus!`.
4. `de-a2-u07-l4-the-doctor-says` **"The Doctor Says…"**: `Ich soll viel schlafen.`, `Du
   sollst nicht joggen.`, `Ich habe eine Erkältung.`, `Ich bin erkältet.`, `die Grippe`,
   `Ich habe mich verletzt.`, `Ich bin krankgeschrieben.`, `Bei welcher Krankenkasse sind
   Sie?` (resolves A1 native-review item 5).
5. `de-a2-u07-l5-back-in-training` **"Scenario: Back at Training"**: a player comes back to
   the club after hurting a foot; the team wins or loses a match: `der Fuß`, `das Spiel`,
   `gewinnen`, `verlieren`, `Ich freue mich!`.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Unit `de-a2-u07`, title "Health and Sport", description "Talk about sport and how you
   feel, give advice to friends, and say what the doctor told you.", in level A2 after
   `de-a2-u06`, with these lessons in order:
   1. `de-a2-u07-l1-sport` "Sport"
   2. `de-a2-u07-l2-how-do-you-feel` "How Do You Feel?"
   3. `de-a2-u07-l3-drink-water` "Drink Water!"
   4. `de-a2-u07-l4-the-doctor-says` "The Doctor Says…"
   5. `de-a2-u07-l5-back-in-training` "Scenario: Back at Training"
2. All of **CDS-1 to CDS-19** pass.
3. Reflexive verbs appear in at least 6 exercises with at least 3 different reflexive
   pronouns, including 1 Perfekt (`Ich habe mich verletzt.`); 1 multiple choice offers a
   missing or wrong pronoun (`Ich fühle gut.` / `Ich fühle mir gut.`).
4. The `du` imperative appears in at least 4 exercises, the `ihr` imperative in at least 2,
   stem-change imperatives in at least 2; 1 multiple choice has the distractors `Trinkst viel
   Wasser!` and `Du trink viel Wasser!`.
5. `sollen` appears in at least 3 exercises with the infinitive at the end.
6. Health content stays everyday and general (rest, water, tea, sleep, seeing a doctor). No
   diagnoses, doses or treatment advice, and no statements about sick-note rules or
   employer duties (CDS-11, PD-051).
7. `Bei welcher Krankenkasse sind Sie?` appears at least once (formal register).
8. Imperative prompts end with "!" in English and are marked "(to a friend)" or "(to
   friends)" so `du` and `ihr` can be told apart (CDS-6).

## Out of scope
- `sich freuen auf / über` (B1), dative reflexive (`Ich wasche mir die Hände`, B1)
- `im Verein`, `ins Bett` (U9)
- Medical advice, emergencies, hospital (B1)

## UX notes
- Tip examples: "Reflexive verbs need a pronoun: Ich fühle mich gut. Er fühlt sich müde.";
  "du imperative: drop -st and du: du trinkst → Trink!"; "sollen = someone else says you
  should: Ich soll viel trinken."
- Keep injuries minor and the tone encouraging.
