# Personas

**Owner:** marketing · **Ticket:** MKT-001 · **Last updated:** 2026-11-20

Four marketing personas built on the pm's segments (`docs/product/market-analysis-german.md`
§2.1) and the numbers in `market-research.md`. They are composites, not real people. The PRD's
product personas (casual beginner, returning learner, try-before-commit visitor) still apply
inside each one.

Each persona lists what Polyglow can honestly offer **today (v0.5.3)** and what they will
miss, so copy and proposals stay truthful.

---

## 1. Priya, the pre-arrival skilled worker (primary)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Segment** | S1 skilled workers (also covers S5 students preparing to move) |
| **Profile** | 27, software tester in Pune, India. Applying for jobs in Germany, considering the opportunity card. English at work, Hindi and Marathi at home. Mid-range Android phone, mobile data |
| **Why German, why now** | More German means a better application and an easier start; she wants A1 done in about three months and A2 by the time she moves. India issued the most opportunity-card visas so far [market-research S8] |
| **Jobs to be done** | Learn on the commute and at lunch; know her level; not waste money before she has a job offer |
| **Today she uses** | Free videos, a gamified app's free tier, a weekend batch class she may drop |
| **Pains** | Data and storage, ads, free tiers that stop her after a few lessons, not knowing if she is "really A1" |
| **Willingness to pay** | Low now (est. INR 100–200 a month); higher once she has a deadline |
| **What Polyglow gives today** | Free, no account, installs from the browser, works offline after loading; A1 and A2 on a CEFR path with checkpoints; correct answers earn hearts; Practice is free |
| **What she'll miss** | Recorded audio on every device, speaking, a target date, B1 |
| **Channels** | Instagram and YouTube Shorts, Reddit, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Telegram groups for Germany jobs, student-visa forums |
| **Message that lands** | "German for your move to Germany. Five minutes a day, free, works offline." |

## 2. Sam, the expat who's already here (primary, in-country)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Segment** | S6 expats in English-speaking jobs |
| **Profile** | 34, product manager from the US, living in Berlin for 18 months. Works in English. Bought a course once and quit after three weeks |
| **Why German, why now** | Tired of being the person who can't read the letter from the Bürgeramt or talk to the neighbour. Thinking about permanent residence later |
| **Jobs to be done** | Handle real-life situations (appointments, doctor, post, train delays); a habit that survives a busy week |
| **Today he uses** | A gamified app on and off, a translation app, colleagues |
| **Pains** | Lessons about tourism, not about his life; guilt about broken streaks; subscriptions he forgets to cancel |
| **Willingness to pay** | Medium–high (est. €5–10 a month) if it clearly helps with real situations |
| **What Polyglow gives today** | A1 and A2 units on registering an address, the doctor, appointments, letters and offices, home and neighbours, train and hotel, work; a tip before every lesson; offline on the U-Bahn |
| **What he'll miss** | Sync between phone and laptop (no accounts yet), speaking, B1 |
| **Channels** | Reddit (r/germany, r/berlin, r/German), expat newsletters and Facebook groups, LinkedIn, Meetup language exchanges |
| **Message that lands** | "The German you need for the Bürgeramt, the doctor and your neighbours." |

## 3. Amara, the spouse with an A1 deadline

| | |
|---|---|
| **Segment** | S3 spouses and family reunification (~72,500 spouse visas in 2024 [market-research S9]) |
| **Profile** | 30, in Lagos, Nigeria, joining her husband in Hamburg. Has an exam date in four months. Studies alone, mostly in the evening |
| **Why German, why now** | She needs A1 for her plans and knows many people fail the first attempt [S9] |
| **Jobs to be done** | Drill every day; see that she's improving; not lose a costly exam fee |
| **Today she uses** | An institute course in the evening, a prep book, YouTube |
| **Pains** | Cost of a retake, no one to practise with, unclear what "A1" means in practice |
| **Willingness to pay** | Medium for a short, clear period (est. a one-off €10–20 "until my exam") |
| **What Polyglow gives today** | All of A1 (60 lessons, ~700 exercises) free; an A1 checkpoint; daily goal and streak |
| **What she'll miss** | A goal date, mock checkpoint with timing, speaking and writing (exams test four skills) |
| **Channels** | Facebook groups for spouse visas and family reunification, WhatsApp, YouTube, course teachers' recommendations |
| **Message that lands** | "Practise A1 every day, between your classes. Free, on your phone." Never promise a pass (PD-035) |

## 4. Tom, the hobby and heritage learner (secondary)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Segment** | S7 heritage and S8 hobby learners |
| **Profile** | 52, teacher in Ohio, German grandparents, planning a trip to Bavaria. Has a long-ago school German |
| **Why German, why now** | Family history, travel, a brain habit |
| **Jobs to be done** | Something pleasant to do daily; skip the very basics he still knows |
| **Today he uses** | A gamified app (frustrated by the new limits), podcasts |
| **Pains** | Being forced to restart at zero; ads; limits on free practice |
| **Willingness to pay** | Medium (est. $30–60 a year) for an ad-free, unlimited experience |
| **What Polyglow gives today** | Start at A2 if he likes ("Where do you want to start?"), unlimited free Practice, no ads, no account |
| **What he'll miss** | Stories and culture content, leagues with friends |
| **Channels** | Reddit r/German, Facebook genealogy and German-American groups, newsletters, Product Hunt-style discovery |
| **Message that lands** | "Pick up your German where you left it. No ads, no account, practise as much as you like." |

## Persona priority

| Priority | Persona | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Priya (pre-arrival, India-first) | Largest growing English-speaking group; Android and offline fit; A1–A2 matches her next 6–12 months |
| 2 | Sam (expat in DACH) | Content fits his life today; reachable through free community channels; higher willingness to pay |
| 3 | Amara (A1 deadline) | Very clear need; A1 complete; best fit for a later "goal date" and paid "pass" |
| 4 | Tom (hobby/heritage) | Large audience, cheaper to reach in English-language communities, but lower urgency and higher churn |

Out of scope for marketing now: learners whose main language is Ukrainian, Arabic, Turkish or
Dari (UI is English only), children under 13 (PRD §2), teachers and schools (until a B2B
offer exists).
