**Title:** I made a free German course app focused on everyday life in Germany (A1–A2). Feedback welcome

Hi all. I've been building Polyglow, a free web app with short lessons for English speakers
learning German. It's organised by CEFR level (A1 and A2 so far, 130 lessons) and the topics
are things like making appointments, the doctor, registering your address, letters from
offices and train trips.

A few design choices you might care about:
- every lesson starts with a short grammar tip
- correct answers give hearts back, and replaying finished lessons is free
- it works offline and needs no account
- both "du" and "Sie" from the start
