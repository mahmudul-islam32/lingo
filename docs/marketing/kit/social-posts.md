### 1. Reddit, r/German (maker post, asking for feedback)
**Title:** I made a free German course app focused on everyday life in Germany (A1–A2). Feedback welcome

Hi all. I've been building Polyglow, a free web app with short lessons for English speakers
learning German. It's organised by CEFR level (A1 and A2 so far, 130 lessons) and the topics
are things like making appointments, the doctor, registering your address, letters from
offices and train trips.

A few design choices you might care about:
- every lesson starts with a short grammar tip
- correct answers give hearts back, and replaying finished lessons is free
- you can set a goal date ("A2 by March") and it shows how many lessons are left and whether
  your daily goal is enough
- it works offline and needs no account
- both "du" and "Sie" from the start

It's new, the content hasn't had a native-speaker review yet, and listening uses your device's
German voice for now. I'd really value corrections and honest feedback. APP_URL

**New in Polyglow: Review**
Practice now has a Review card. It brings back up to 10 exercises from lessons you've already
played: the ones you found tricky, and the ones you haven't seen for a while.
Right answer? It comes back later: after 1 day, then 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.
Not quite? It's ready again right away.

Free, no daily limit, and it works even with 0 hearts. Every correct answer gives a heart back,
and review XP counts for your daily goal and streak.

Already learning with Polyglow? Your review list starts with the lessons you finish after this
update. APP_URL
